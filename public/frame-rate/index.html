Is GTA 6 30 or 60 fps?

Updated November 11, 2026

Hands-on previews reported GTA VI running at 1440p with a 30 frames-per-second target on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not confirmed a 60 fps mode.

By console

ConsoleWhat is known
PS5Previews: 30 fps target at 1440p
Xbox Series XPreviews: 30 fps target at 1440p
Xbox Series S30 fps target reported. Resolution on this console has not been detailed
PS5 ProListed as PS5 Pro Enhanced. The actual improvements have not been confirmed

Why not 60?

Technical analysts quoted across the games press expect 30 fps because of the processor in current consoles. A world this dense leans on the CPU, and the PS5 Pro uses the same CPU as the standard PS5, so its stronger graphics chip does not solve that limit. This is analysis, not a statement from Rockstar.

Could 60 fps come later?

Possibly, but nothing has been announced. Any site that promises a 60 fps patch date is guessing.

Get the smoothest picture you can

  • Switch your TV to Game Mode. It cuts the delay between pressing a button and seeing the result.
  • Turn off motion smoothing. It adds delay and visual glitches in games.
  • Use an HDMI port that supports your TV's best features if your set has only some ports marked for gaming.
