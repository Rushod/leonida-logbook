GTA 6's hidden stat boost missions: six side missions that permanently raise health, stamina and Focus

By Adrian R · November 28, 2026

Gyms aren't the only way to get stronger in GTA VI. Six hidden side missions give a permanent boost to max health, stamina or Focus, and one raises all three. How to start each one, and what you get.

Our stats guide covers the slow way to train Jason and Lucia: gyms, running and the shooting range. There's also a quicker way. A handful of hidden side missions give a permanent boost to max health, stamina or Focus when you finish them. None of them has a map marker until you trigger it, so most players find them by accident.

GamesRadar+ has played through six of them and published a list on November 27. Windows Central and GTABase have separately written up The Final Flush. Here's how to start each one. We've kept the endings inside spoiler boxes.

Alien abduction: all three stats

This is the best one. At night, look for a car by the road with its doors open and lights flashing. GamesRadar says it can appear in more than one place, and its writer found one near Port Gellhorn. Follow the trail from the car, use Focus to spot a light in the trees, pick up the item there, then go back to the car. You'll be abducted and wake up by the road. The car gets impounded at the nearest police station, so don't use one you care about.

To finish it, sleep with the same character for a day or two until the dreams start.

Spoiler: how the abduction mission ends

The dreams are a set of puzzles: a keypad code (random each time, so write yours down) and a four-light panel. Avoid the middle door. Back in the real world, go to the radio tower above Shoeless Keys, enter the code from your dream, solve the light panel again and finish the rescue. Move quickly near the end, because gas drains your health. The permanent health, stamina and Focus boost comes when the rescue is done, not when you wake up.

The Final Flush: stamina

Find a "Stop, Don't Flush!" poster in a public restroom and scan its QR code with the WAiNK app. GTABase lists three spots: the men's room at the Corsair Key restroom block (the easiest early on), the Watson Bay campground restrooms, and the ladies' room at the Nine1Nine club in Vice City. Reply to the message, then go to Frank's Waffles in Tequesta at night. During the day the icon shows a padlock. GTABase says only one of the two protagonists can do it, and the stamina boost goes to whoever does.

Spoiler: what happens in The Final Flush

The man at the diner, Dr. Merle, drives you to a sewage plant on Gloriana Key. You get inside, set three lights to green on a control panel to open a staircase, and reach the chamber he's after. The plant's alarm gives you a one-star wanted level, and you wake up at your safehouse.

Crop dusting: stamina

In Chapter 2, scan the crop-dusting poster outside the reception of the motel. It sends you to McKittrick Farm. Dust the crops, then come back later when the farm asks you to collect chemicals.

Spoiler: how it ends

You eat one of the mutated strawberries. This is the "Hulk-like" side quest in our easter eggs roundup.

Circles of Hell: health

Around midnight, look for an open garage in Port Gellhorn lit up in red. Go inside with the people there. As well as the health boost, you get a shotgun and a T-shirt.

Spoiler: what's in the garage

The mission ends with a fight against "the devil".

Mushroom obstacle course: stamina and a tattoo

Go to the logging yard at Lake Tallaloko, north of Milliport, and climb into the yellow shipping crate. GamesRadar's writer found it open, but says a reader needed a cutting tool to get in. Finishing the course gives a stamina boost and a new tattoo.

Iguana shooting: Focus and $250

In the same area as GamesRadar's abduction car, follow the sound of gunfire into the woods next to a road construction site. Win the $250 bet. You'll get your money back plus $250 and a permanent Focus boost. GamesRadar recommends the Duke 556 assault rifle.

How sure is this?

GamesRadar's writer played each mission and describes the rewards first-hand. The Final Flush also matches Windows Central's and GTABase's walkthroughs. Rockstar hasn't published a list, and GamesRadar expects to find more, so there may be other stat boost missions. Meditation spots also raise Focus, and they're on our collectibles page.
