GTA 6 sold an estimated 19 million copies by launch morning, but there's no official number yet

By Adrian R · November 19, 2026

Market data firm Sensor Tower puts GTA VI at about 19.1 million copies and $1.84 billion by the morning of November 19. Take-Two has not shared figures.

Update, November 22: Take-Two has now released official figures: $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million copies sold before release. Read our report on the official numbers.

The first sales number for GTA VI is out, and it is not from Rockstar. Market data firm Sensor Tower estimates the game had sold about 19.1 million copies, worth about $1.84 billion, by the morning of launch day. Reuters reported the figure first.

What the estimate says

  • About 19.1 million copies sold by the morning of November 19, counting pre-orders.
  • About $1.84 billion in revenue, more than $1.5 billion of it from pre-orders, according to 80 Level's summary.
  • Roughly three in four copies were on PlayStation, the rest on Xbox.

Is it official?

No. Sensor Tower estimates sales from store and market data, and Take-Two and Rockstar have not released a number. Take-Two has only said that pre-orders were tracking far above its expectations. Its CEO, Strauss Zelnick, said on an earnings call earlier in November, "we still aren't ready to declare victory." Some reports suggest official figures could come within days, but Take-Two has not given a date.

How it compares with GTA V

GTA V sold 11.21 million copies and made $815.7 million in its first 24 hours in September 2013, a Guinness world record at the time. The GTA VI estimate is already well above that, but the two numbers are not measured the same way: GTA V's covers a full day of sales, while GTA VI's is mostly pre-orders counted before most of the world could play.

Why it matters

If the estimate is close, GTA VI already had the biggest launch on record before most countries had even reached their unlock time. Take-Two has since shared its own numbers (see the update above). Still deciding on a console? See which consoles run GTA VI.
