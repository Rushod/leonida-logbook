Rockstar explains two GTA 6 design choices: why there's no mission replay, and what the Criminal Profile is for

By Adrian R · November 25, 2026

GTA VI's story missions can't be replayed, and your Criminal Profile can lock you in as Irredeemable. Two Rockstar leads have now explained why the game works this way.

Two of GTA VI's biggest changes from GTA V have come up again and again in player complaints and questions this week. Rockstar has now explained the thinking behind both.

Why you can't replay missions

GTA V let you replay any story mission and chase gold medals. GTA VI doesn't: once a mission is done, it's done. Kotaku asked Rockstar about it, and senior design director Scott Butchard said by email that replays were "looked at early on in development", but that the feature felt at odds with the story as the game came together. On adding it later, he said "we do not currently support it", which isn't a promise either way.

Replays first arrived in GTA IV's Ballad of Gay Tony and came back in GTA V with medals for optional goals. Kotaku sees GTA VI's missions as closer in spirit to Red Dead Redemption 2's slower, more role-playing style.

What it means for you: manual saves are the only way back to an earlier point, so save before big missions. See how saving works and the missions overview.

Why the Criminal Profile exists

Rockstar co-founder and development lead Rob Nelson told Eurogamer, in an interview GamesRadar relayed on November 25, that the Criminal Profile is GTA VI's answer to Red Dead Redemption 2's honour system. His point: in GTA V, people on the street barely reacted unless you pointed a gun at them, leaving you "just sort of this demigod stomping through the world". GTA VI's NPCs notice how you play, and Nelson says having choices other than violence is more fun over a long game.

That's why going on rampages pushes your rating toward Psycho and, if you keep it up, the permanent Irredeemable rating, which changes how people react to that character. The Brief's summary of the interview gives an example of the other side: threaten someone with a gun and they may simply do what you want, no beating needed.

What it means for you: if you like causing chaos, mix in some calm play before your rating locks. Our Criminal Profile guide explains each rating and how to check it.
