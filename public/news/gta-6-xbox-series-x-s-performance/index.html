GTA 6 on Xbox: Series X holds up, Series S drops to 720p and below 30 fps

By Adrian R · November 18, 2026

The first Xbox Series X|S tests are in. Series X looks close to PS5, while Series S renders at 720p and dips toward 20 fps in heavy action.

This morning the Xbox versions were the big gap in GTA VI's launch coverage. Digital Foundry and Xbox-focused reviewers have now filled it in. The short version: Series X is in good shape, and Series S works but makes real sacrifices.

Xbox Series X

  • Resolution: renders at 1440p and upscales to 4K, the same starting point as PS5.
  • Frame rate: a 30 fps target that Pure Xbox's reviewer found largely smooth, with the occasional dropped frame. BGR, rounding up reviewer testing, puts the worst moments in the mid-20s.
  • Small issues: Pure Xbox noticed some pop-in and a handful of minor bugs, nothing that got in the way.

Xbox Series S

  • Resolution: renders at 720p and upscales to 1080p. Rockstar told Wccftech it uses AMD's FSR 4 upscaler for this.
  • Frame rate: Digital Foundry found everyday shootouts and car chases running around 25 fps, bigger scenes nearer 20, and one explosive firefight dipping to 18 or 19. Quieter moments, walking around and cutscenes, usually hold 30.
  • What you lose: according to BGR's summary of the tests, rougher or missing shadows, more pop-in at a distance, a shallower depth-of-field effect and lower-detail textures on things like clouds and water. Rockstar says the lighting itself matches the other consoles.

So which console is best?

Digital Foundry still rates the PS5 Pro as the best version, with a near-flawless 30 fps. Series X sits roughly alongside the standard PS5. Series S is very playable, but if you are sensitive to frame drops it is the version to avoid if you have a choice. BGR's verdict is that the game is still worth playing on Series S, with compromises.

Can I get a better result on Series S?

There is no performance mode or graphics setting that changes this on console. Turning on Game Mode and switching off motion smoothing on your TV helps with responsiveness, and our frame rate guide has the full table for every console.

None of this includes a patch yet. Rockstar's 1.002 update arrived before launch with no patch notes, and no launch-day performance fix has been announced.
