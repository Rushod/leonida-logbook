GTA 6 doubled UK games retail sales in launch week, but it isn't GTA V in the shops

By Adrian R · November 26, 2026

NielsenIQ's UK figures for GTA VI's launch week: £142 million of games retail, £32 million of boxed GTA VI copies, and a rush on controllers and SSDs. Why the boxed number ranks only 17th all-time.

The first UK sales data for GTA VI is in. NielsenIQ, which tracks what UK shops sell, published its launch-week numbers on November 26, covering November 19 to 21. They only count boxed games, consoles and accessories sold at retail. PlayStation Store and Xbox Store downloads aren't included.

The numbers

  • £142 million spent on games retail in the UK that week, up 99% on the week before.
  • £43 million of that was boxed games, up 409% and the biggest week for physical games since Christmas 2018.
  • £32 million was boxed GTA VI, about 74% of all boxed game sales that week. 88% of it sold on launch day.
  • GTA VI also went straight to No.1 on the UK all-formats boxed chart, according to Games Asylum, ahead of EA Sports FC 27.

Why it's "only" 17th

GTA VI's first week of boxed sales ranks 17th among all UK game launches since 1995. GTA V is still first, and GTA IV is 11th. That isn't a sign the game is selling badly. Most people now buy digitally, and NielsenIQ's Dorian Bloch points out that boxed games were half of games retail spending in 2008 but only about 15% so far in 2026. GTA VI's boxed copy also contains just a download code. Even so, Bloch says "GTA VI has still made a huge impact."

For the worldwide picture, Take-Two reported $3.79 billion in day-one sales and Sony counted 39 million PS5 players in four days.

Consoles and controllers

  • Console sales were up 38% on the week before: PS5 up 46%, Xbox Series up 75%.
  • Accessories made £21 million, up 123%. Controllers were up 194% (PS5 controllers up 355%), headsets up 94%, and external SSDs up 30%, their best week of 2026.

The SSD jump makes sense: GTA VI takes up to 212 GB. If you're short on space, see our storage guide.
