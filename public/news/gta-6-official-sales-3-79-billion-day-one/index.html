GTA 6 made $3.79 billion on day one, Take-Two confirms

By Adrian R · November 22, 2026

Take-Two's first official GTA VI numbers: $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million copies sold before release, both records. Here's what the figures do and don't include.

We now have official numbers. On November 20, Take-Two said GTA VI brought in $3.79 billion in retail sales on its first day and sold 32.5 million copies before release. The company calls the first figure a record for any entertainment launch, films included.

What Take-Two announced

  • $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales worldwide, based on company estimates up to the end of November 19.
  • 32.5 million copies sold before launch, which Take-Two says is a record on any platform.
  • The game is on sale through the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Games Store and shops. The announcement says nothing about a PC version or an online mode.

What the numbers mean

The $3.79 billion is money spent by players at retail. It isn't Take-Two's revenue, since stores and platform holders take their cut. It's also the company's own estimate, not an audited result. The Hollywood Reporter, as cited by Make Tech Easier, says the total includes pre-orders. Take-Two didn't say how much came from digital and how much from boxed copies.

How it compares

In 2013, Take-Two said GTA V sold more than $800 million on its first day. That makes the GTA VI figure about five times bigger. Earlier this week we reported Sensor Tower's estimate of about 19 million copies by launch morning. Take-Two's 32.5 million is well above that. That estimate was made before launch day ended and was built from market data rather than Take-Two's own sales records, so the two numbers measure different things.

What's next

Take-Two hasn't said when it will share an updated total, and Rockstar still hasn't announced a PC version, an online mode or the first post-launch patch. We'll report those when they're official. For what's in the game right now, see our mission walkthrough.
