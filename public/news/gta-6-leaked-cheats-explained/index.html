Those "leaked" GTA 6 cheat codes are GTA V's. Here's what we know

By Adrian R · November 11, 2026

Viral posts claim GTA 6 cheat codes have leaked. The lists are GTA V's codes, and Rockstar has not confirmed any cheats for GTA VI.

If your feed is full of "GTA 6 cheat codes may have just leaked" posts this week, here is the short answer: the codes in them are from GTA V, and none has been confirmed for GTA VI.

What the viral lists actually show

The posts going around show familiar names: PAINKILLER for invincibility, BUZZOFF for an attack helicopter, phone numbers starting 1-999. Those are GTA V's cheats, released in 2013. Even the posts themselves usually admit in the caption that nothing is confirmed.

What Rockstar has said

Nothing. Rockstar has not said whether GTA VI has cheat codes at all, or how you would enter them.

Will GTA V's cheats work in GTA VI?

Probably not as they are. Rockstar usually changes cheats between games, and GTA VI changes things the old codes relied on. There is no weapon wheel, for example, which makes a "give all weapons" cheat work differently. What could return is the idea: an in-game phone exists, and Rockstar used phone numbers for cheats in GTA V.

How we will handle it on launch day

  • From November 19, we test GTA V's codes in GTA VI and mark each one as working or not.
  • Any real GTA VI cheat gets added with the button inputs for PS5 and Xbox.
  • Nothing goes on the list until we have seen it work.

Until then, every GTA V code with PS5, Xbox and PC inputs is on our cheats page, ready to try.
