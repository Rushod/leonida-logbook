GTA 6 sold an estimated 25 million copies on day one

By Adrian R · November 20, 2026

Analyst firm Sensor Tower estimates GTA VI sold about 24.6 million copies and took about $2.36 billion in its first day. Take-Two has not confirmed any numbers yet.

The first sales figures for GTA VI are in, but they are estimates, not official numbers. Market tracker Sensor Tower, which counts pre-orders and digital sales, puts the game at about 24.58 million copies sold by the end of launch day, worth roughly $2.36 billion.

The numbers, as estimated

  • Copies: about 24.6 million, including pre-orders.
  • Revenue: about $2.36 billion.
  • By console: around three quarters on PS5 (about 18.4 million), and about 6.2 million on Xbox Series X|S.

How that compares

GTA V sold about 11.2 million copies and made over $815 million in its first day in 2013, then passed $1 billion in three days. If Sensor Tower is right, GTA VI more than doubled both in a single day, and out-earned the opening weekend of Avengers: Endgame.

Why we call it an estimate

Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has released sales figures. As VGC puts it, the publisher has not "officially confirmed day one sales". Take-Two usually reports numbers with its quarterly results, so official figures may take a while. We will update this post when they arrive.

What it means for players

Very little day to day: the game is the same however many copies it sells. For what's in the game so far, see our first look, and for how it runs on your console, our Xbox test round-up.
