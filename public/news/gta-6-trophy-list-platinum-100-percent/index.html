GTA 6 trophy list: 55 trophies, and the platinum needs 100% completion

By Adrian R · November 19, 2026

GTA VI has 55 trophies and achievements, all single-player, worth 1,000 Gamerscore. The platinum needs 100% completion, which guides put at about 200 hours.

Trophy hunters, clear your calendar. GTA VI's full list of trophies and achievements is now public on PS5 and Xbox, and the platinum asks for everything.

The list at a glance

  • 55 in total: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze on PS5. On Xbox the same list is worth 1,000 Gamerscore.
  • All single-player. There are no online trophies, since GTA VI has no online mode at launch.
  • About half are hidden. GTABase counts 28 secret entries. GamesRadar+ warns that some of their names give away mild story spoilers, so we're not listing them here.
  • The platinum needs 100% completion. The trophy for reaching 100% is itself one of the two golds. Push Square's verdict: "Strap in for the long haul."

What counts toward 100%

GTABase's breakdown is the most detailed so far. Some things must be done in full, others only in part:

  • All of them: robberies (including the bank vaults), underwater discoveries, a gold medal at every shooting-range challenge, and every drone race.
  • Most of them: scanning 200 of the vehicles, studying 100 of the animals, a set number of optional missions, stunts and challenges.

Side trophies worth knowing about

  • Master of None: try each of the 9 activity types once.
  • Top Fun: win a drone race at all 14 locations.
  • Positive Mental Attitude: meditate at all 18 spots. GTABase says one of them may be missable early in the story, but hasn't confirmed it.
  • Whip It Good: discover 100 vehicles.
  • Cunning Stunts: complete 250 stunts.
  • Maximum Withdrawal: rob five bank vaults and get away.
  • Stick Fiddler: beat an arcade game.

How long will it take?

GTABase estimates about 200 hours for the platinum, in line with Red Bull Gaming's early guess of more than 200 hours for 100%. One more warning: using a cheat turns trophies off for that session.
