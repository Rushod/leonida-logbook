GTA 6's first post-launch patch is out: update 01.002.002, with no patch notes yet

By Adrian R · November 23, 2026

Rockstar has started rolling out GTA VI's first update since launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Here's the version, the download size, how the staged rollout works and what we know about what it fixes (not much).

Update, November 23: Rockstar has now published patch notes for this update. It lists seven fixes and no performance changes. Read the full list.

Four days after launch, GTA VI has its first post-launch update. It started rolling out on November 23 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, according to VICE, Geo News and LibertyCity. It's the third update overall, after the two pre-launch patches on November 13 and 16.

The basics

  • Version: 01.002.002 on PS5. VICE and LibertyCity report the Xbox version as 1.0.2.2.
  • Download size: about 604 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S, as reported by VICE and LibertyCity. Geo notes it isn't clear whether the sizes are the same in every region.
  • Platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. There's still no PC version, so there's no PC patch either.

Why you might not have it yet

On PS5 the update is going out in waves, not to everyone at once. All three outlets describe the same schedule: about a third of players first, about two thirds roughly three hours later, and everyone about six hours after the start. The outlets think this is to spread the load on PlayStation and Xbox servers, but Rockstar hasn't said why. Reports disagree on whether Xbox is also staged: VICE says only PS5 is, while Geo says both are.

If it hasn't arrived on PS5, LibertyCity suggests checking by hand: highlight GTA VI on the home screen, press Options and choose Check for Update.

What does it fix?

We don't know, and neither does anyone outside Rockstar. As VICE put it, Rockstar "hasn't released patch notes" for this update, and it published none for the two pre-launch patches either. Rockstar Support (support.rockstargames.com) is where official notes would appear.

Geo lists launch-week problems the patch might target, including an Xbox Quick Resume glitch that reportedly unlocks the frame rate but speeds up the whole game, PS5 crash error CE-108255-1, HDR calibration problems and the accidental first-person camera. That's Geo's guess, not a confirmed changelog, so don't assume any of these are fixed until Rockstar says so or players test it. For the bugs reported so far and what to do about them, see our launch bugs report.

Should you install it?

Yes. GTA VI is single-player and you'll need the latest version for future updates anyway. If you're mid-mission, finish it or reach a save point first, since the game has to close to apply the update. Rockstar has since published the notes: here's what it fixes.
