GTA 6's in-game trading cards are becoming real Panini cards in December

By Adrian R · November 24, 2026

Panini and Rockstar Games have announced an official GTA VI Sports Trading Card Collection based on the fictional athletes you collect in the game. Here's what's in a box, the rare parallels, and what isn't known yet.

The trading cards you find around Leonida are getting a physical version. On November 24, Panini America announced the GTA VI: Sports Trading Card Collection, made with Rockstar Games. It recreates the game's fictional sports stars and teams, and VGC calls it the first officially licensed Grand Theft Auto trading card set. The news came from Panini's blog and social accounts, which tag Rockstar. We haven't seen a separate Rockstar Newswire post.

What's in a box

  • Four packs of four cards, so 16 cards per box (Panini's product page, via RockstarINTEL and Heavy).
  • On average each box has two Rookies, one Retro, one Illustrated and four Special Silvers.
  • Colour parallels: Silver, Pink, Teal and Light Blue.
  • Numbered Legend parallels: Bronze (49 copies), Silver (25) and Gold (one of one).
  • Sample players named so far include Dartanyon Pearson, Carl Kasey and Maleek Rivers.

What isn't known yet

Panini says the set comes out in December 2026 but hasn't given an exact date, a price, pack odds or a list of shops. VGC notes that "there isn't currently a way to place a pre-order". Panini's product page lists it as coming soon. We'll update this post when pricing is out.

The cards in the game

In GTA VI, trading cards are one of the collectibles you pick up around the map, and rare ones can be sold at pawn shops. Dexerto reports 480 cards across 18 sets, covering sports stars, comic characters and nightclub dancers. The Panini set covers only the sports side.
