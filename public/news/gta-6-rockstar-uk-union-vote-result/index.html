Rockstar's UK staff vote against union recognition, a week after GTA 6's launch

By Adrian R · November 27, 2026

Rockstar's UK developers rejected a recognition deal with the IWGB union by about three to one, on a 93% turnout. The numbers, what Rockstar and the union said, and the tribunal case still waiting on a ruling.

The ballot ran across GTA VI's launch, and the result came in on November 26: Rockstar's UK staff voted against formally recognising the IWGB Rockstar Game Workers Union.

The numbers

  • 1,163 against (75.3%) and 381 for (24.7%), out of 1,544 votes (IGN).
  • Turnout: 93.2%.
  • Who voted: staff at Rockstar's five UK studios in Edinburgh, Leeds, Lincoln, Dundee and London. The ballot ran for two weeks (Engadget).

What it means

Without a recognition agreement, Rockstar doesn't have to negotiate with the union or hold regular meetings with its representatives, Engadget explains. The union still exists, and staff can still join it.

What both sides said

Jennifer Kolbe, Rockstar's head of publishing, said in a statement to IGN and the Press Association that the result reflects the team's support for keeping "direct relationships, collaboration and open dialogue". Before the vote she had told Bloomberg that if staff wanted a union, they would have one.

The IWGB said the result "isn't a sign to retreat" and that it will keep organising for pay transparency, paid overtime written into contracts, and protections against AI and layoffs.

The tribunal case

The campaign grew after Rockstar fired a group of UK workers in October 2025. Rockstar says they shared confidential details about the game and made disparaging comments. The union says they were fired over union activity. Outlets give different numbers for how many workers were fired: IGN says 31, and The Herald says 23 former workers are part of the case. An employment tribunal in Glasgow heard closing submissions this month, and it hasn't ruled yet. We'll update this post when it does.

This doesn't change anything in the game, its patches or its support. We're reporting it because it's news from the studio that made GTA VI.
