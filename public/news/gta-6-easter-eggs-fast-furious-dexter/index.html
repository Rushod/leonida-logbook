GTA 6 easter eggs found so far: a Fast & Furious car, Dexter's flat and a literal egg

By Adrian R · November 23, 2026

Players have spent launch week hunting Leonida for secrets. Here are the GTA VI easter eggs outlets have picked up, what's confirmed, and a first-person camera trick Rockstar didn't add on purpose.

It took players less than a day to start finding Rockstar's in-jokes in Leonida. Most of what's circulating comes from clips on X and Reddit, so we've only listed the finds that outlets have reported, and we say how solid each one is. Rockstar hasn't commented on any of them.

Brian O'Conner's 1995 Eclipse

The best-supported find so far. GamesRadar and BGR both report that a green 1995 Mitsubishi Eclipse in the style of Paul Walker's car from the first Fast and Furious film sits in a garage on Vespucci Island. BGR, citing Vice, places it in a garage attached to a white house near the middle of the island. GamesRadar says you have to break into the garage to take it, and that it's in every version of the game. The in-game badge isn't Mitsubishi, since GTA uses its own brand names, and we haven't seen a reliable report of what the game calls it.

A Dexter-style apartment

GamesRadar reports an apartment complex on Washington Island in Vice Beach that recreates Dexter Morgan's home from the TV series, down to a boat called "Slice of Life" and tools on the roof. This one comes from a GamesRadar video and player posts, so treat the exact details as reported rather than tested by us.

The egg on the tower

IBTimes UK picked up a player video from November 18 showing a bird's nest full of coloured eggs at the top of a communications tower, reached after a climb of about seven minutes. Throw an egg and the game cuts to a short scene of it hitting a car windscreen below. The tower's location hasn't been pinned down, and IBTimes is clear that this is still a player report, not a documented feature.

Two nods to George R.R. Martin

GamesRadar reports two Game of Thrones jokes that players posted on X. A book on Jason's bedside table, "The Battle for Zantoroth" by "T.D.D. Ewell", has a cover in the style of Martin's novels. And a news ticker reports a man who died after drinking gas to "become a dragon", which GamesRadar links to a death in the Dunk and Egg stories. Both come from player posts that GamesRadar relayed, so they're reported, not tested by us.

Comics and films

  • RX Men: Reddit players spotted a comic called RX Men, with a Magneto-like villain called Dr. Placebo. GamesRadar reported it.
  • Hulk: GamesRadar says a side quest about strawberries ends with a character turning into something very Hulk-like.
Film nods in later story missions (tap to show)

GamesRadar's writer compares some later missions to the heist and siege set pieces of Heat and Die Hard. That's the writer's own comparison, not something Rockstar has said.

A first-person camera, by accident

GTA VI has no first-person mode. GamesRadar reports a glitch that gives you one anyway: press the camera view button repeatedly while putting the phone away. It works better than you'd expect, with some visual glitches, but the only way back to the normal camera is to reload a save. Save first if you want to try it. GamesRadar also points out that GTA V only got its first-person mode in a later re-release, so a proper one could come later. Rockstar hasn't announced one.

Found one?

We'll add easter eggs to our map once their locations are confirmed by more than one source. For now, the Eclipse garage is the only find with a location backed up by more than one outlet.
