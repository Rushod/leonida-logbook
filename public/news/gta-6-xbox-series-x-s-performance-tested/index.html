GTA 6 on Xbox, tested: Series X holds 30 fps, Series S struggles

By Adrian R · November 20, 2026

Digital Foundry's Xbox tests are out. Series X runs much like a standard PS5 at about 1440p, while Series S drops to 720p and often falls below 30 fps.

Digital Foundry has now tested GTA VI on Xbox, filling the gap left by its PS5 coverage. The short version: Series X is a solid way to play, and Series S is the weakest version of the game.

Xbox Series X

  • Frame rate: 30 fps, held through normal play and even busy shootouts. It dips in the heaviest chases and firefights.
  • Resolution: a fairly fixed 1440p, so the image is a little soft next to PS5 Pro.
  • Versus PS5: very close to the standard PS5. Digital Foundry found it slightly smoother on a 120Hz TV with variable refresh rate turned on.

Xbox Series S

  • Frame rate: 30 fps is the target, but it slips under that for much of normal play. Driving and everyday combat sit in the mid to upper 20s, and the worst explosions drop it to about 20, briefly as low as 18 to 19.
  • Resolution: about 720p inside the game, upscaled for your TV, with less precise lighting, softer reflections and messier shadows.
  • What still works: ray-traced reflections and hair are kept, and walking around and cutscenes usually run fine.

Digital Foundry summed it up this way: Series S "often lies in a 25-30fps no-man's-land".

Which console is best?

PS5 Pro, which holds 30 fps almost perfectly and has the sharpest picture and ray-traced shadows. Series X and the standard PS5 come next and are close. Every console is capped at 30 fps, with no performance mode. See our frame rate guide for the full table.

Will a patch fix Series S?

Rockstar has not said. Digital Foundry hopes for improvements but is unsure how much is possible on the console. Treat any claim of an upcoming Series S fix as unconfirmed until Rockstar announces it.
