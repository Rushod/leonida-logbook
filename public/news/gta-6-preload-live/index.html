GTA 6 preload is live: start times, 212 GB and how to make room

By Adrian R · November 11, 2026

Digital GTA VI pre-orders can download now on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and boxed copies follow on November 12. What to do before launch.

You can now download GTA VI ahead of launch. Digital preloading opened on November 10, two days earlier than an early pre-order listing suggested.

When preload opened

VersionPreload starts
PS5, digitalNovember 10, midnight in your local time zone
Xbox Series X|S, digitalNovember 10, midnight GMT everywhere (7pm ET on November 9)
Boxed copiesNovember 12, once you redeem the download code inside

Preloading does not let you play early. The game stays locked until it unlocks in your region. See what time GTA 6 unlocks.

212 GB, and that is before the patch

Rockstar says the download needs up to 212 GB depending on your console. A day-one update will add more. On a 512 GB Xbox Series S, that is well over half of the usable space.

Do these three things tonight

  • Check your free space and clear room. The storage guide shows how much each console really has.
  • Start the download early. At these sizes, a slow connection can take a full day. Step-by-step instructions are in the preload guide.
  • Leave the console in rest mode with internet access on, so it keeps downloading and grabs the patch.
