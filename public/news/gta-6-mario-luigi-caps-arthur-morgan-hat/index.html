GTA 6 hides Mario and Luigi caps in Lummox County, and Arthur Morgan's hat is at a yard sale

By Adrian R · November 25, 2026

Two of GTA VI's best hidden extras are things you can wear: a secret block in the hills that drops J and L plumber caps, and a $35 cowboy hat straight out of Red Dead Redemption 2. Here's where to find both.

Not every GTA VI secret is a three-day puzzle. Two of the nicest ones this week are simply hats, and both are easy enough to go and get yourself.

The Mario and Luigi caps

A player on X, xGarbett, found a Nintendo-style secret in the hills of Lummox County in northern Leonida. Kotaku's Zack Zwiezen then went there and confirmed it works, and TweakTown, Nintendo Life and Beebom have all covered it since. Here's how it works, going by those reports:

  • Where: a group of ruined brick and stone buildings at the end of a winding road past Mount Kalaga (Beebom places it south of Moreau). Look for a purple tarp in the ruins.
  • The trigger: there's a bong on the tarp. Use it, then climb the ruins and stand in the plume of smoke rising from one of the columns. An invisible block appears above you.
  • Hit the block: jump into it from below. A short tune plays and two caps pop out. xGarbett says he couldn't break it by jumping at first and shot it instead, so try that if jumping doesn't work for you.
  • The reward: a red cap with a "J" for Jason and a green cap with an "L" for Lucia, a clear nod to Mario and Luigi. One outlet's image caption lists different colours, so don't be surprised if yours look slightly different.

Nobody has said yet whether the caps join your wardrobe for good or only last until you change outfits. We'll update this when that's tested.

Arthur Morgan's hat

Red Dead Redemption 2 fans should check the yard sales. Gfinity and Beebom report that a yard sale in southern Port Gellhorn during Chapter 2 sells a "Genuine Cowboy Hat" for $35, a lookalike of Arthur Morgan's. It's near the couch at the sale. If you buy it early in Chapter 2 only Lucia can wear it, and Jason can too once you're able to switch between them later in the chapter. It's an optional purchase, not part of a mission.
