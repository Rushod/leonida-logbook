GTA 6 side secrets: a jet ski dog, a missing persons case and an NPC who thinks he's in a game

By Adrian R · November 23, 2026

Three GTA VI side secrets picked up by outlets this weekend: how to meet Cool Dog at sea, where to start the Missing Persons case in Port Gellhorn, and the Belville garage covered in simulation theories.

Launch week's first batch of easter eggs was mostly references to films and TV. The newest finds are things you can actually go and do. GamesRadar has tested the first two, and the third comes from a player post that VICE picked up.

Cool Dog, the jet ski rescue

Get stranded far out at sea, away from the coast and other boats, and you may be picked up by a dog on a jet ski. GamesRadar says the game calls him Cool Dog, and its writer found him by drifting out from Leonida Bay. Players report meeting him in other places too.

  • Wait. It can take several minutes. You'll hear barking, then music as he arrives.
  • Press Triangle / Y to climb on. Holding L2 / LT lets you try to talk to him, though he doesn't say much.
  • He takes you to a beach where other dogs live. You can play fetch with a mini football and throw discs.
  • When you're done, Cool Dog knocks some branches off a boat and uncovers a wallet with over $1,200 in it, according to GamesRadar.

Missing Persons in Port Gellhorn

This is a proper side case with three bodies to find, and GamesRadar has played it through. It opens in Chapter 2. Start at the reception of the Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn, where there are three missing person posters, then look for a dome-shaped warehouse north-east of Moses' garage. Don't use the shutter. Crawl in through a gap on the north-east side, go through the trophy room, and check the three lockers for photos of the missing people.

  • Susanne Keeble: in the trunk of a pink car with the plate PRTTYGRL, in the junkyard behind the Port Gellhorn Pay 'N' Spray.
  • Patrick Murray: under the floor of an abandoned trailer in the trailer park north of the water tower in north-east Port Gellhorn. A dog barks at the door.
  • Donald Miller: in a sunken car in a lake by a tree stump, on the border of Port Gellhorn and Wild Hog Point, south-west of town.

Report each one to the Kelly County Missing Persons Tip Line. GamesRadar's call back only came later, at night, after it had played on to the Chapter 2 mission Breakfast Run.

How the case ends (tap to show)

The tip line caller asks you to meet at the warehouse. GamesRadar says the killer is Alvan West. If you go alone, he drugs and kidnaps you, and your partner has to rescue you from an old mine near the Kalaga Reservoir in Mount Kalaga National Park, then chase him down. If Jason and Lucia go together, you face him together instead. There's no cash reward, but it improves Jason and Lucia's relationship.

The man who thinks he's in a simulation

VICE reports a house in Belville, a small town near Lake Leonida, whose garage is covered in a corkboard with red string and notes such as "IT'S A SIMULATION" and "WHO IS L, WHO IS J?". Brochures suggest the owner wants to buy a boat and sail past Leonida's edge, which VICE links to Cool Dog. This comes from a player's post on X that VICE relayed. VICE gives no exact address, and we haven't checked it ourselves.
