GTA 6 does have cheats: 61 of them, and you have to beat the story first

By Adrian R · November 19, 2026

GTA VI's cheats are entered on the phone with a D-pad code, unlock after the main story, and turn off trophies and saving. The 11 codes two guides agree on so far.

Good news for anyone who spent launch week typing GTA V codes into the wrong game: GTA VI does have cheats. Bad news for anyone hoping to spawn a tank on day one: you have to finish the main story before any of them work.

Guides from GamesRadar+ and Red Bull Gaming, both updated since the game unlocked, agree on how the system works. Rockstar itself has not published a list.

How cheats work in GTA VI

  • 61 cheats in five groups: player, combat, vehicle spawns, environment and screen filters.
  • No phone numbers and no PC-style typing. Press Up on the D-pad to take out the phone, hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox), then enter a D-pad sequence. The codes are the same on PS5 and Xbox.
  • They are earned. Every cheat stays locked until the Jason and Lucia story is complete. Most then need a challenge or stat as well, such as shooting-range medals, workouts or shopping challenges. GamesRadar+ says the Cheats list sits in the pause menu's Progress tab.
  • There's a price. Activating a cheat turns off trophies and achievements, and saving, for the rest of that session.

Codes found so far

Most codes are still unknown: GamesRadar+ lists 19 of 61, and its list is still growing. We have added the 11 codes that both guides give identically to our cheats page, including the $250,000 money cheat, Infinite Ammo and Body Armor. We have not tested them ourselves yet, so we mark them as reported rather than verified.

Do the GTA V codes work?

No. GTA VI uses its own sequences, and the old phone numbers do nothing. That matches what we said about the "leaked" lists last week.

Why it matters

Locking cheats behind the ending makes them a post-game toy rather than a shortcut. With the main story at 91 missions (see our missions page), most players will not see a single cheat for weeks.
