GTA 6 on Xbox tested: Series X matches PS5, Series S runs at 720p and drops to 20 fps

By Adrian R · November 19, 2026

Digital Foundry's Xbox results are in. Series X looks and runs much like a base PS5. Series S renders at 720p and often sits below 30 fps.

The last gap in the launch performance picture is filled. Digital Foundry has now tested GTA VI on Xbox Series X and Series S, and the two consoles have very different stories.

Xbox Series X: about the same as a PS5

  • Resolution: about 1440p internally, the same as PS5.
  • Frame rate: a 30 fps target it mostly holds, with dropped frames in especially busy chases and firefights.
  • Verdict: VGC sums up DF's view as performing similarly to the standard PS5. PS5 Pro is still the best place to play.

Xbox Series S: the weakest version

  • Resolution: 720p internally by DF's counts, output at 1080p. Pure Xbox reports Rockstar told Wccftech it uses AMD's FSR 4 upscaling, and DF says the final image looks a lot better than 720p suggests.
  • Frame rate: cutscenes and walking are usually fine, but slow driving sits in the mid-to-upper 20s for long stretches, typical combat holds in the mid-20s, and the hardest fights fall to about 20 fps.
  • What's cut: it keeps ray-traced reflections and detailed hair, but lighting is less precise, reflections are softer, shadows messier, and clouds, water and draw distance are lower quality.
  • Verdict: DF's Oliver Mackenzie said he'd "advise players to play the game on other console platforms for now."

What if Series S is all you have?

  • It's still playable. DF admires how much of the game survives, and it is the same GTA VI, just rougher.
  • Turn on VRR if your TV has it. Variable refresh rate can make small dips feel less jarring. That's general advice: DF's results don't cover it.
  • A fix is not promised. Rockstar has not announced a performance patch, and VGC says it's unclear how much Series S can improve after launch.
