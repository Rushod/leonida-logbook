GTA 6 does have cheats, but you have to finish the story first

By Adrian R · November 21, 2026

GTA VI has cheat codes after all. They unlock only once the main story is done, each has its own goal, and using one stops you saving.

Good news and bad news for anyone hoping to spawn a tank on day one. GTA VI does have cheats, entered with button codes on the phone like in GTA V. But none of them work until you have finished the main story.

How it works

  • Finish the story first. Every main mission with Jason and Lucia has to be done. PC Gamer tried the codes on a save that had not finished the campaign, and they did nothing.
  • Then unlock each cheat. Each one has its own goal, shown in a Cheats menu: open the map, go to the Progress tab and scroll all the way right. Locked cheats show a padlock.
  • Enter them on the phone. Press Up on the D-pad to bring up the phone, hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox), then put in the directional code.
  • Five groups: player, combat, vehicle spawns, environment and screen filters.

The catch: no saving

Using a cheat stops you saving for the rest of that session, according to both PC Gamer and Insider Gaming. Insider Gaming also says it switches off trophies and achievements. That is stricter than GTA V, where you could still save. Unlocks also belong to one save file, so starting a new game resets them.

How many are there?

Early guides, led by PowerPyx and reported by Insider Gaming, count 61 cheats. Rockstar has not published a list, and some codes in those guides are still unconfirmed.

Example unlock goals (light spoilers for side content, tap to show)
  • $250,000 cash: available once the story is done.
  • Body armor: 100 workouts at the gym. See our gym guide.
  • Tank: complete every survival challenge.
  • Inflatable flamingo: destroy 50 cocaine flamingos.
  • "Golden Hour" screen filter: spend four hours holding hands with a partner.

Is it worth the wait?

For a first playthrough, it changes little: you were going to play the story anyway. Cheats here are a reward for the post-game sandbox, not a shortcut. Our cheats page explains the system and adds codes to its verified table only once they are confirmed working.
