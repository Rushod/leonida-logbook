GTA 6's two hidden playlists: how to unlock The Sniper of La Perle and The Vault

By Adrian R · November 27, 2026

GTA VI has two secret playlists you unlock by scanning posters, and you need both for 100% completion. Where the five posters are, and how to listen once you've found them.

GTA VI's 100% checklist has a line called Unique playlists, and it isn't obvious how to get them: they don't appear in the radio menu until you find them. There are two, and you unlock both by scanning posters with your phone. Your progress is under Progress, then Collections, then Exploration, then Unique Playlists.

The Sniper of La Perle: four posters

This one is a 22-track playlist made up mostly of Haitian and Creole-language music. VICE reports it's hosted by Florida rapper Kodak Black, and it includes several of his songs. Scan all four posters (locations from PowerPyx's guide):

  1. Aunt Zola's Soul Food, La Perle: inside the restaurant, by the cashier.
  2. TWA Ponya Tattoo, La Perle: on the table next to the entrance.
  3. Drip Drops, La Perle: on the counter next to the clerk in the clothing store.
  4. Jack of Hearts, Crosstown: on a crate in the eastern recording booth on the club's second floor.

The game doesn't show which posters you've already scanned, so if it hasn't unlocked after four, go back and scan each one again. When it unlocks, you'll find it under On Demand, in a car or on Jason or Lucia's earbuds.

The Vault: one poster

Go to The Vault nightclub in Downtown Vice City and scan the red poster just inside the entrance. The playlist then appears in the radio list as The Vault.

The poster locations come from PowerPyx's collectibles guide, which was updated November 23. VICE gives the same four locations for The Sniper of La Perle. Rockstar hasn't published an official list.
