GTA 6 in Japan is a censored version: no nudity, less blood and fewer languages

By Adrian R · November 26, 2026

GTA VI's Japanese release covers up nudity, tones down blood and has fewer language options than the global version. What players in Japan have found, and what stays the same.

GTA VI is rated CERO Z (18+ only) in Japan, and the Japanese release isn't the same game everyone else is playing. Kotaku and VGC both reported the differences on launch day, based on screenshots, clips and streams from players in Japan and a breakdown by Japanese site GameSpark. Rockstar hadn't announced any regional changes before launch and hasn't commented since.

What's different

  • No nudity. Jason and Lucia shower in underwear or swimwear, and extra clothing is added to beach and sex scenes. Kotaku says some scenes are cut entirely.
  • Less blood. There's less of it, it fades faster, and unlike other regions there's no setting to turn it up. VGC, citing GameSpark, says decapitations are removed too.
  • Fewer languages. According to Kotaku, Japanese copies offer English voices with English or Japanese subtitles. Copies sold outside Japan don't include Japanese subtitles at all.

What stays the same

VGC describes the changes as cosmetic: the missions, the map and how violent the gameplay is all match the international release.

The smaller download was the clue

Before launch, Automaton reported that the Japanese download was about 1.2 GB smaller (208.7 GB against roughly 209.9 GB on PS5), and that Japan's rating listed violence and crime but not sexual content. Kotaku now says the missing data "turns out to be mostly made up of nudity".

If you're buying in Japan

  • Japanese boxed copies contain a download code that, according to Kotaku, has to be redeemed within 170 days.
  • Need Japanese text? Going by Kotaku's report, only the Japanese version has Japanese subtitles, so a copy bought abroad won't help.
