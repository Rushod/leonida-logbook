GTA 6 patch 01.002.002 notes are out: seven fixes, nothing for performance

By Adrian R · November 23, 2026

Rockstar has published notes for GTA VI's first post-launch update. It fixes crashes, autosave, stuck wanted levels, story phone calls and more, but nothing about frame rate or the Xbox Quick Resume glitch.

Earlier today we reported that GTA VI's first post-launch update, 01.002.002 on PS5 and 1.0.2.2 on Xbox Series X|S, was rolling out without any patch notes. That's changed. Rockstar has now added the notes to its GTA VI launch updates page on Rockstar Support, dated November 23, and KeenGamer, Apple Scoop and others have reported the same seven fixes in the same order.

What the update fixes

All seven are listed under "General / Miscellaneous". In our words:

  • Crashes: several issues that could crash the game are fixed. Rockstar doesn't name any error codes.
  • Weather: fixed weather that stayed the same for long stretches.
  • Autosave: fixed the game not autosaving properly.
  • Phone calls: fixed calls that didn't come through, including ones that move the story on or unlock missions. If your progress stalled waiting for a call, this is the fix to look for.
  • Wanted level: fixed a bug where you couldn't lose the police.
  • Gym: fixed the deadlift bar not being usable.
  • Loot bags: fixed partners not handing over bags, which stopped you selling them to a fence.

What it doesn't mention

As KeenGamer and Game News Plus point out, the notes list no performance, frame rate or graphics changes. That means nothing about:

  • The Xbox Quick Resume trick. Pure Xbox and GamesRadar reported a glitch where switching display modes and using Quick Resume lifts the 30 fps cap on Xbox Series X|S, but ties game speed to frame rate, so everything runs too fast. Rockstar hasn't said whether it will stay or go.
  • PS5 error CE-108255-1. Some players say the update stops it, but the notes don't name it, so treat that as unconfirmed.
  • The accidental first-person camera and the heavy screen vignetting some players have complained about. Neither is in the notes.

There's still no 60 fps mode on any console, and Rockstar hasn't announced one. See our frame rate page for the tested numbers.

How to get it

The PS5 version goes out in waves, so it can take up to about six hours to reach everyone. If you don't have it yet, highlight GTA VI on the home screen, press Options and choose Check for Update. Reported sizes are about 604 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox. For the rest of the launch problems players are reporting, see our launch bugs report.
