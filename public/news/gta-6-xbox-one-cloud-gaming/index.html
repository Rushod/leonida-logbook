GTA 6 is coming to Xbox One after all, through cloud streaming

By Adrian R · November 11, 2026

Microsoft says Xbox One owners can stream GTA VI through Xbox Cloud Gaming from November 19 at 1am ET. You still have to buy the game.

Xbox One owners are not locked out of GTA VI after all. Microsoft announced on November 10 that the game will stream through Xbox Cloud Gaming from launch day, so it plays on the 2013 console even though it was never built for it.

The key facts

  • When: streaming starts on November 19 at 1am ET.
  • Where: Xbox One, plus Xbox Series X|S. On Series X|S, Microsoft recommends playing the installed version for the best picture.
  • You must buy the game. Microsoft says GTA VI is purchased separately, so streaming does not make it free.
  • Picture quality: the stream uses Xbox Series S graphics settings. Microsoft has not given a resolution or frame rate.

Do you need Game Pass?

Microsoft's post does not say which subscription GTA VI streaming needs. It does say that from November 11, players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime to stream supported games without Game Pass, but it does not confirm GTA VI is one of them. Check the game's page on your console before launch day.

Who this helps

  • Xbox One owners who are not ready to buy a new console.
  • Series S owners short on space. Streaming needs no download, while the installed game needs up to 212 GB. See the storage guide.

The catch

Streaming depends on your internet connection. A slow or busy connection means a blurrier picture and input lag, which matters in a shooter. On launch night, demand for the servers will also be at its peak.

A bonus most people missed

Microsoft's post also lists what comes with a pre-order made before November 19 at 23:59:59: the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+, which has to be redeemed within 180 days. See Standard vs Ultimate for the full contents.
