GTA 6 is out in New Zealand: what players are finding inside the game

By Adrian R · November 18, 2026

Kiwi players got GTA VI first, at 11:00 UTC on November 18. Here's what the first hours show, from the prologue to Snapmatic, with spoilers kept behind a warning.

The 13-year wait is over in at least one country. GTA VI unlocked at midnight on November 19 in New Zealand, which was 11:00 UTC on November 18 (6am Eastern), and streams from the first players filled X and Twitch within minutes. Everyone else unlocks at their own local midnight. See your exact unlock time.

The basics, now confirmed in the released game

  • Platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC version at launch.
  • Single-player only. There is no online mode on day one. See what we know about online.
  • Download: up to 212 GB. New Zealand's main broadband network operator, Chorus, called the size unprecedented, and said one download is more than a quarter of what an average home uses in a month.

What the opening hours look like (no story spoilers)

  • It starts with a prologue. A flashback doubles as the tutorial, teaching you to move, handle a weapon and survive an on-foot police chase.
  • Jason's first mission covers a lot of ground. It mixes in stealth and puts you at the controls of a boat and then a plane before you ever reach a road and a car.
  • The game shows how Jason and Lucia met early on, and yes, it involves a stolen car.

Snapmatic is huge, and it is laughing at us

The phone's Instagram-style app, Snapmatic, holds hundreds of posts, videos and comments, and new ones appear as the story moves on and Leonida reacts to what you have done. One early post has already gone viral: a character complains that a game based on a kids' cartoon came out before "Race and Chase 7". Race'n'Chase was GTA's original working title, so it's Rockstar's joke about everyone who spent years saying they got something before GTA 6.

Story spoilers from the prologue (tap to show)
  • The game opens with Lucia in prison. You can talk to other inmates before she is called to see her counsellor, which leads into the flashback.
  • The flashback is the armed convenience-store robbery that put her there. She runs, and the police tackle and taser her.
  • The big surprise: Jason and Lucia have a child, who was waiting in a nearby coffee shop during the robbery and has been taken by child protective services. None of the trailers showed this.

The "New Zealand trick": does it work?

  • Xbox: players on Reddit say the game unlocked after they set their console's location to New Zealand. Microsoft and Rockstar have not said whether that will keep working.
  • PS5: changing settings does not work. You would need a separate New Zealand PlayStation account and a second copy bought from that store.
  • Bans: posts claim some accounts were suspended, but none has been confirmed. Separately, Twitch reportedly warned a German streamer that using the trick on stream could lead to a ban.

What we still don't know

Proper performance tests on Xbox, how bad any launch bugs are (update: here's what players are reporting), and whether a day-one patch changes anything. Critic reviews and Digital Foundry's PS5 test are already out: here is what they say.

As GTA VI unlocks around the world, we are checking our map, cheats, missions and collectibles against the finished game. Anything we confirm gets marked as verified.
