How vehicles work

  • Fuel. Cars and motorcycles have a fuel gauge, and electric vehicles use charging stations.
  • Handling. Heavier than GTA V, and you cannot steer a flipped car back onto its wheels.
  • Types. Supercars, off-roaders, boats, kayaks and electric scooters are all reported.

Owning a car

  • A stolen car is temporary until you register it. Unregistered cars eventually disappear.
  • You start with three garage slots and can buy more garages across Leonida.
  • The Scooter Bros app has a driver bring your personal vehicle to you.
  • Abandoned personal vehicles are impounded. You can pay a fee to get them back.

Stealing a car

  • Older cars open with a slim jim or a smashed window. Modern cars need a key cloner or phone hacking tool.
  • The WAINK phone scanner shows a car's locks, value, alarm, tracker and registration cost.
  • High-end cars have trackers that alert police. A fence removes them.

Shops

  • Pay & Spray repaints, repairs, values and registers cars.
  • Mod Shops each offer their own modifications, including nitrous.
  • The trunk stores extra weapons, gear and spare outfits.
