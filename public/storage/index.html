How much storage does GTA 6 need?

Updated November 11, 2026

GTA VI needs up to 212 GB of free space, depending on your console. That makes it one of the largest console games so far.

212 GB against each console

Percentages use the 212 GB maximum. The exact size per console has not been published, and the day-one update is extra.

How to check your free space

  • PS5: Settings, then Storage, then Console Storage.
  • Xbox Series X|S: Settings, then System, then Storage devices.

Ways to make room

  • Delete games you have finished. Your saves stay on the console or in the cloud, and you can download the game again later.
  • Move older games to a USB drive. PS4 and Xbox One games can run from an external USB drive, which frees the fast internal storage.
  • Clear captures. Saved video clips can take tens of gigabytes.

If you need more storage

GTA VI has to run from fast storage. A normal USB drive can hold the game but cannot run it.

  • PS5: add an M.2 NVMe SSD to the expansion slot inside the console. Sony recommends one with a heatsink.
  • Xbox Series X|S: plug a Storage Expansion Card into the port on the back.
