How to switch between Jason and Lucia

Updated November 21, 2026

Hold Right on the D-pad and press R1 / RB. Free switching unlocks near the end of Chapter 2; until then the story picks who you play.

The buttons

  • Switch: hold D-pad Right to open the partner menu, then press R1 (PS5) or RB (Xbox).
  • Bring your partner to you: hold D-pad Right and press Triangle / Y. They drive over to you.
  • Hold, don't tap. A quick tap of Right sets off explosives you've placed.

The partner menu works on foot, in cars, on boats and in aircraft. It also shows Jason and Lucia's relationship.

When can you switch?

Not at first. You play Jason through Chapter 1, with Lucia only in short story sections. Free switching opens near the end of Chapter 2: Breakfast Run teaches it and Coffee Break, which follows straight on, opens it up in free roam. Guides name one or the other, so finish both.

Together or apart

  • Together: the swap is instant. In a car it moves you between the driver's and passenger seats.
  • Apart: the camera pulls up over the map and drops down to the other character.
  • Free chauffeur: set a waypoint, switch to the passenger seat, and your partner drives you there.

Why can't I switch?

  1. You're not far enough in. Finish Breakfast Run and Coffee Break.
  2. The mission is locked to one character. Some story and side missions are Jason-only or Lucia-only; the game says so before they start. From Making Gainz until VCIA Takedown, the pair also can't be seen together in public.
  3. Your partner is busy. A greyed-out portrait means they're doing something, such as riding in a RydeMe. Wait a moment and try again.
  4. You tapped instead of held D-pad Right.

You can't finish the story as just one of them: both have required missions. See the walkthrough for who plays each one.
