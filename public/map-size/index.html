How big is the GTA 6 map?

Updated November 11, 2026

The state of Leonida is reported to be about three times the size of Red Dead Redemption 2's map, which would make it Rockstar's largest world so far.

The numbers reported from previews

  • Whole map: about 3 times the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2.
  • Vice City: about 2 times the size of GTA V's Los Santos.
  • Built-up land: roughly 11 times the urban area of GTA V, counting the smaller towns.
  • Roads: about 15% narrower than in GTA V.

These figures were relayed by people who attended Rockstar preview events. Rockstar has not published official measurements.

How the map works in the game

  • Areas you have not visited are hidden until you explore them.
  • The map screen uses satellite-style imagery.
  • Fast travel uses the RydeMe taxi app, buses and a working train line.
  • Previews report a very large number of buildings you can enter.
