GTA VI map of Leonida

Official

Rockstar's official map of Leonida

Rockstar released this printable map of Leonida as a free download on its official GTA VI site on launch day. It splits the state into five counties: Lummox in the north, Kelly in the west, Leonard in the east, Vice-Dale around Vice City and Mariana across the south and the Keys.

The interactive map above uses the community's detailed map, because the official one is a poster rather than something you can zoom into. The two line up: the community's launch-day update took its names from the released game.

About this map

Where this map comes from

Rockstar doesn't publish a map of Leonida, so this one uses the GTA VI Mapping Community's hand-traced map. It was built over several years from trailers and screenshots, and updated on launch day (version 18) with names and places from the released game.

  • About 970 named places: landmarks, stores, bars, marinas, airports, racetracks and more. The launch-day update added about 50 new ones and corrected names like Henry J Edwards Airport and Southpoint Airfield.
  • Markers are labelled "Community" until we check them ourselves in the game.
  • Found markers are saved in your browser, so you can tick places off as you explore.

Looking for something specific? See fence locations, Ammu-Nation stores and how to clear the map fog.
