GTA VI map of Leonida

About this map

A pre-launch map, clearly labelled

GTA VI is out on November 19, 2026. Until then, no one has an official map of Leonida, so this one uses the best community reconstruction available: the GTA VI Mapping Community's hand-traced map, built over several years from trailers, screenshots and other pre-release material.

  • Every marker is speculative today. The 900+ named places come from the same community project and have not been checked in the game.
  • From launch day, locations we check in the released game are marked Verified, and missions, collectibles, vehicles and weapons are added.
  • Found markers are saved in your browser, so you can track your progress as you play.
