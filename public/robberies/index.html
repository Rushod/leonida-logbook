Making money

GTA 6 robberies: how to make money

Updated November 11, 2026

Every store in Leonida can be robbed, and bigger scores include jewelry stores, small banks and gang compounds. How you do the job changes the payout.

Based on hands-on previews. Every point gets re-tested in the finished game on launch day.

Store robberies

  • Get in quietly. A lock-picking tool called the Snap Gun opens doors.
  • Deal with the cameras. You can destroy CCTV footage so it does not identify you.
  • Reach the back room. Clerks who run can drop the keys.
  • Wear a mask before you start, so witnesses and cameras cannot record your face.

Bigger scores

TargetWhat to know
Jewelry storesHigher value than ordinary stores
Small banksMay pay in marked banknotes, which need a specialist fence
Gang compoundsListed among the high-value targets

Speed or greed

  • Blow the safe: fast, but explosives lower the payout.
  • Find the combination: full payout, but it takes longer and police may arrive.
  • Carry more bags: larger bags hold more loot, and your vehicle's storage sets the limit.

Turning loot into cash

  • Stolen goods and stolen cars are sold to a fence. Damage lowers what a car is worth.
  • High-end cars carry trackers that must be removed at a fence before the car can be sold or registered.
  • Cash goes into the account Jason and Lucia share once you deposit it at an ATM. Getting arrested costs you the cash you are carrying.
