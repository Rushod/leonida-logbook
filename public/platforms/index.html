Can you play GTA 6 on PS4, Xbox One or PC?

Updated November 11, 2026

GTA VI launches on November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Xbox One owners can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming. There is no PS4 or PC version.

Things to know before buying a console for it

  • There is no disc. Boxed copies contain a download code, so a console without a disc drive works fine.
  • You need an internet connection to download the game.
  • Storage matters. A 512 GB Xbox Series S has about 364 GB of usable space, and GTA VI can take 212 GB of it. See the storage guide.

Is it on Game Pass or PlayStation Plus?

We have found no announcement that GTA VI will be included with either subscription at launch. Plan on buying it. See Standard vs Ultimate.
