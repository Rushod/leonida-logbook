GTA 6 wanted level: how it works and how to lose it

Updated November 11, 2026

Police in GTA VI only come after you when a crime is seen, reported or caught on camera, and they chase a description of you that you can change.

Based on hands-on previews. Every point gets re-tested in the finished game on launch day.

How you get stars

  • A witness sees the crime, the victim reports it, or a security system records it. No witness, no stars.
  • The maximum is six stars. One star is fairly easy to escape.
  • Hollow stars mean police know a crime happened but not who did it. They turn solid once you are identified.

What police know about you

Officers build a profile of your face, outfit, weapons and vehicle, and the screen shows which parts they have. Police do not normally appear as dots on the minimap during a chase, so you have to spot them yourself.

How to lose a wanted level

  • Break line of sight and get out of the search area around the crime until it cools down.
  • Change your look. Swap outfits, or change your hair at a salon.
  • Ditch what they identified. Drop the weapon or leave the car they have on record.
  • Switch to an ordinary vehicle to pass roadblocks without being noticed.
  • Use Pay & Spray to repaint a car mid-chase.
  • Split up. Separating Jason and Lucia drops the active player's level by one star.

Before the crime

Wearing a mask stops cameras and witnesses from identifying your face in the first place.

What happens if you are busted?

Getting arrested costs you the cash you are carrying. Money already deposited at an ATM is safe, which is a good reason to bank it before a risky job.
