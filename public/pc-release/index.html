Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Updated November 11, 2026

Not at launch. GTA VI releases on November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and no PC version or PC date has been announced.

What Take-Two has said

In a May 2026 interview with Bloomberg, Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick explained the console-first plan by saying Rockstar is judged on serving its core audience first. He did not give a PC date.

What past Rockstar games did

  • GTA V reached PC 19 months after its first console release.
  • Red Dead Redemption 2 reached PC about 12 months after consoles.

If GTA VI follows the same pattern, a PC version would arrive a year or more after November 2026. That is an estimate from history, not an announcement, and reports that name a specific month are unconfirmed.

Can you play it on a PC screen anyway?

If you own the game on a console, Remote Play can stream it from your PS5 or Xbox to a computer on a good connection. The game still runs on the console.

Should you wait for PC?

  • Wait if you only game on PC and are happy to avoid spoilers for a year or more.
  • Buy a console if you want to play at launch. See which consoles run GTA VI.
