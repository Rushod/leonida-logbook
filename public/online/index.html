Does GTA 6 have online at launch?

Updated November 11, 2026

No online mode has been announced for launch day. Official listings describe GTA VI on November 19, 2026 as a single-player game.

What is confirmed

  • Take-Two's pre-order announcement and the PlayStation Store listing describe GTA VI as a single-player experience.
  • Rockstar has not given a date, a name or any details for a GTA VI online mode.
  • The launch download does not include the next GTA Online instalment.

What is only reported

In September 2026, Bloomberg reported comments from Twitch chief executive Dan Clancy suggesting Rockstar would launch multiplayer the following year. Rockstar has not confirmed that, so treat 2027 as a report and nothing more.

What happens to GTA Online?

Rockstar has not announced any shutdown of the current GTA Online, which runs on GTA V. For now it remains the way to play Grand Theft Auto with other people.

Can you play with a friend at all?

Not in the way previews describe it. The teamwork shown so far is between the two story leads, Jason and Lucia, who you switch between. See how GTA VI works.
