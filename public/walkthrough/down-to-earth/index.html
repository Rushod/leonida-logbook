GTA 6 Down to Earth walkthrough

Updated November 21, 2026 · All missions

Down to Earth is mission 14 of 22 in Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise. You play as Lucia. Here's every step, the choices you can make and what to watch out for.

Chapter1
Mission14 / 22
CharacterLucia

Where it starts

Brian's place. Follow him into the marina.

Step by step

  1. Clean Brian's boat with the pressure washer. R2 / RT fires it like a gun.
  2. Brian sends you on a pick-up. Fly the plane to Bobcat Bay.
  3. Land near the boat and collect the cocaine.
  4. The deal goes wrong. Kill or knock out the dealers on the boat.
  5. Fly to the next drop and land on the water.
  6. Fly to the last drop and make it alongside a moving boat.
  7. Land back at Brian's dock. Lucia's parole officer turns up at the house.
  8. Hide the drugs in the shed, inside the washing machine.
  9. Talk to the parole officer with Lucia.

Tips

  • Use L2 / LT a lot as you come down onto the water. Hit too hard and the plane explodes, failing the mission.
  • Land a bit short? You can taxi the plane across the water like a boat.

After the mission

Missable: the next morning Lucia offers to meditate with you on the balcony. Say yes. You can't do it after Chapter 1.
