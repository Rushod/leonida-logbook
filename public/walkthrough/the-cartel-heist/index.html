GTA 6 The Cartel Heist walkthrough

Updated November 21, 2026 · All missions

The Cartel Heist is mission 26 of 26 in Chapter 3: Tits Ass Drug Money. Here's every step, the choices you can make and what to watch out for.

Chapter3
Mission26 / 26
Before you start: This is the last mission of Chapter 3 and a long one. Make a manual save first. Practice the safe steps from Safe Word if you're rusty: you'll use all three.

Where it starts

Once Hidden Charges and the other Chapter 3 missions are done, meet Raul at the planning room in Stockyard. After the briefing, get in the black car with Lucia and Nicole.

Step by step

  1. Drive downtown, park in the marked spot and set off the tower charges.
  2. Drive to Sinfrontera National Bank. With all three towers down you have about 11 minutes before the police are free.
  3. On Karim's signal, knock out the marked guard and herd every customer and staff member into the middle of the lobby, side rooms included. Stop anyone Karim says is heading for the door first.
  4. Lucia: take the elevator down with Raul and Nicole. Threaten Lex into the security room, keep pushing until he opens the door for the truck, then knock him out when he moves to the wall.
  5. Jason: take down the guards from the elevators and upper floor. Ride from the second floor to the roof, clear it, climb the ladder and secure the backup terminal.
  6. Lucia: crack the vault. Drill (both bars move this time), thread the camera cable round the sharp right turn to the gold hook, then hold the door while Nicole drills.
  7. Bag the big stacks of bills first.
  8. Hold off the police. As Jason, defend the second floor and swap the shotgun for something with range. As Lucia, cover the left side, then regroup with Raul in the middle. Stay in cover after the SWAT flashbang.
  9. Lucia gets into the SWAT truck and throws Jason a smoke grenade launcher. Smoke out the police cars in front, and Lucia runs for the bus.
  10. Jason: help Nicole up and follow her right to the stairs, down to the basement (flashbang on the landing), through the left door to the delivery bay and up the driveway. Take the Brigade SWAT truck at the top of the ramp.
  11. Lucia: shoot chasers out of the back of the bus, listening for Raul's warnings. When the roof comes off, Raul hands you a grenade launcher with unlimited ammo. Hit the helicopter when it's right behind you, or shoot the pilot in Focus.
  12. Jason: ram police cars with the armored truck, pull up on the bus's left and jump across. Drive the bus down the freeway and keep left when Raul says.

Tips

  • Shoot guns out of guards' hands instead of killing them to keep a Professional rating.
  • Big stacks are worth far more than loose cash. Grab them first.

After the mission

Pays $120,000. Afterward you can stay in the area or go straight to Ocean Beach, the first mission of Chapter 4.
