GTA 6 Girls' Trip walkthrough

Updated November 21, 2026 · All missions

Girls' Trip is mission 4 of 11 in Chapter 2: Paradise Lost. You play as Lucia. Here's every step, the choices you can make and what to watch out for.

Chapter2
Mission4 / 11
CharacterLucia

Where it starts

Vicki's room at the motel.

Step by step

  1. Talk to Vicki and buy painkillers for Jason.
  2. Take them back to Jason. They're fake.
  3. Go back to Vicki's room. Lucia kicks the door in; Vicki explains she's a former addict and can't keep the real thing at home.
  4. Drive Vicki's car to Delights, the strip club where she works.
  5. Wait while Vicki collects her pay, then talk to Frankie Delight.
  6. Drive to Dunk's trailer. He isn't home, so head to the boatyard.
  7. Dunk's friends in the old warehouse say he went to the car wash.
  8. At the car wash, open the shutter. The Kolts gang inside turn hostile as Dunk runs.
  9. Chase Dunk toward the gun range.
  10. Find him in the far paddock, to the right of the gun range's main building.
  11. Put Dunk in your car and drive him home. He says he'll have pills soon.

Choices

  • After Lucia knocks Vicki down, help her up or leave her. She gets up either way.

Tips

  • Don't wreck Dunk's car during the chase or the mission fails.
  • You can't really catch Dunk before he crashes at the gun range, so just stay close.
  • Hitting the bong at the motel is optional, but turning it down makes you look like a cop.
