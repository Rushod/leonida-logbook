GTA 6 Gone Fishing walkthrough

Updated November 21, 2026 · All missions

Gone Fishing is mission 2 of 22 in Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise. Here's every step, the choices you can make and what to watch out for.

Chapter1
Mission2 / 22

Where it starts

Jason and Cal waiting on a fishing boat in the Keys.

Step by step

  1. Follow Donnie's plane as it drops packages into the water. Ease off the throttle near each one so Cal can hook it out.
  2. Pick up every package. They're all marked on the map.
  3. Follow Donnie to his meeting with the Kingfishers gang and watch through binoculars.
  4. When the meeting goes bad, park near the Kingfisher camp and go in on foot.
  5. Sneak through the camp using the tall grass on the right. Crouch with Circle / B, grab an enemy with R2 / RT and take them down with Square / X.
  6. Near the building, press R3 / RS to use Focus and pick out the target.
  7. Go in through the unlocked front door and untie Donnie. A big Kingfisher attacks: hit with Square / X and dodge with X / A.
  8. Grab the Zombix he drops. It's a health item: use it with L1 / LB and Square / X.
  9. Get Donnie out and run for his plane on the dock. You only need to reach it, not kill everyone.
  10. Fly over the Keys and land at Brian's Marina.
  11. Drive Cal home, then go back to Jason's house and step inside. Lucia's phone call ends the mission.

Choices

  • Stealth or guns. You only carry one weapon, so sneaking is much easier.
  • On the way out, a marked trailer has cash on a table. An enemy jumps out first. You can skip it and head for the gate.

Tips

  • There's a hammer behind Donnie's building if you want a melee weapon.
  • Hang on to Zombix. It's the common healing item for the whole game.
  • Focus (L3 / LS or R3 / RS) works outside missions too, and highlights enemies and useful items.
