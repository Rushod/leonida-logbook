Read this before you start

  • Spoiler-light. Later chapters stay folded so mission names don't give the story away. Open only the chapter you're on.
  • Two points of no return. Tourist Trap (Chapter 1) takes away Jason's first house and the cash you carry, so bank at least $5,000 first. Checking Out (Chapter 2) ends your stay at the motel; have $15,000 saved. Make a manual save before both.
  • No replays and no medals. You can't replay a finished mission, and story missions have no gold medal goals, so nothing in a mission is lost by playing it your way.
  • Nothing for 100% is missable. Everything on the 100% checklist can still be done in free roam after the story. A few optional side jobs can be missed (listed below), but none count toward 100%.
  • Your ticks are saved in this browser and also show on the progress tracker.

Why some lists show more than 91

The in-game story list also includes short tasks, such as moving money into savings or sending money to someone, between the real missions. Counting those gives about 109 entries. We list only the playable missions; the tasks pop up on your phone and are hard to miss.

Mission names and order

Mission names and order follow the launch-week mission lists published by KeenGamer, GTABase and VGC, which agree on the missions. A couple of mid-chapter missions swap places between lists because some can be played in either order. Step-by-step guides for single missions are added as we play them.
