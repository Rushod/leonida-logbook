GTA 6 Checking Out walkthrough

Updated November 21, 2026 · All missions

Checking Out is mission 11 of 11 in Chapter 2: Paradise Lost. Here's every step, the choices you can make and what to watch out for.

Chapter2
Mission11 / 11
Before you start: Point of no return. You can't back out once it starts, and the Starlet Motel stops being your home for good. Have the $15,000 saved and make a manual save first.

Where it starts

Your room at the Starlet Motel.

Step by step

  1. As Jason, take the shotgun from the bed. Use cover outside, clear the parking lot and grab ammo from the bodies.
  2. Cross to the café, where Lucia is waiting.
  3. As Lucia, take cover behind the breakfast bar. Enemies shoot through several windows. Use painkillers or adrenaline and take out the main attackers.
  4. Jason steals a car while a biker goes for Lucia. Focus kicks in on its own: shoot the biker and take his Sanchez.
  5. Ride after Jason using his pink marker on the minimap. Pull alongside his car until the jump prompt appears, then jump in.
  6. As Lucia, shoot at the police while Jason drives. Aim for tires and engines.
  7. The pair arrive in Vice City.

Tips

  • Falling too far behind Jason on the bike can fail the mission. Keep his marker close.
  • Shooting police instead of their cars affects your Criminal Profile.

After the mission

No cash reward. Chapter 3: Tits Ass Drug Money begins in Vice City.
