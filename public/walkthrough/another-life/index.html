Another Life is mission 2 of 25 in Chapter 4: Vice City, Baby. You play as Lucia. Here's every step, the choices you can make and what to watch out for.

CharacterLucia

Where it starts

Straight after Ocean Beach. It's a flashback to Lucia's last morning with Tomas before her arrest.

Step by step

  1. Pick up the phone on the table.
  2. Walk to the room at the end of the hall and lift Tomas out of his crib.
  3. Carry him to the living room and use things around the room to calm him down. Turn up the radio on the counter when prompted.
  4. Follow the prompts to dance around the room with Tomas.

Tips

  • A short story mission with no way to fail.

After the mission

Unlocks the Vice City, Baby! trophy and a BuckMe Black account with a $2,000,000 limit.
