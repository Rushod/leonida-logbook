GTA 6 Afternoon Delight walkthrough

Updated November 21, 2026 · All missions

Afternoon Delight is mission 10 of 11 in Chapter 2: Paradise Lost. Here's every step, the choices you can make and what to watch out for.

Chapter2
Mission10 / 11

Where it starts

Vicki calls with a plan to rob her boss. Go to Delights in Port Gellhorn. You choose whether to start as Jason or Lucia.

Step by step

  1. Arrive at Delights. It's ladies' night, so Lucia walks in the front but Jason needs another way in.
  2. As Lucia, talk to Vicki at the bar. She explains the plan and how to get into the safe in the boss's office.
  3. Switch to Jason outside. Wait for the two bouncers to leave the back fence, or jump the fence to the left of the main door.
  4. Get into the male dancers' dressing room and meet Mario and Lucky. Mario asks Jason to help on stage.
  5. Say yes or no (see Choices).
  6. Get into the back office and open the safe. The code reported so far is 2008.
  7. Empty the safe.
  8. Fight your way out. Security is on full alert.
  9. Escape the bikers chasing you from the club. Killing them makes it easier.
  10. Meet Vicki and hand over the money.

Choices

  • Help: Jason dances while Lucia slips into the office. Security catches her and Jason has to fight the bikers to get her out, so it's a longer route.
  • Don't help: the dancers walk off, Frankie takes the stage, and Jason fires into the ceiling to start the robbery. Shorter.
  • Both routes end the same way and pay the same.

Tips

  • Starting a fight at the entrance fails the mission. Sneak in.
  • Stay quiet until you have the money, or security reacts early.
  • Only one walkthrough has published the 2008 safe code so far. If it doesn't work for you, use the information Vicki gives you at the bar.

After the mission

Vicki sends $10,000 through BuckMe the next in-game day, most of the $15,000 for the move.
