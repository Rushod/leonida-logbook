GTA 6 Coming Home walkthrough

Updated November 21, 2026 · All missions

Coming Home is mission 11 of 22 in Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise. Here's every step, the choices you can make and what to watch out for.

Chapter1
Mission11 / 22

Where it starts

Four errands on the map first, then the prison.

Step by step

  1. Optional errands before pick-up: buy a necklace at the jewelry shop, grab a few items at the grocery store, collect Lucia's clothes from the bar where Andi works, buy flowers, and get Jason a haircut at the barber.
  2. Drive to the prison and talk to the guard. After a flashback, Jason and Lucia are reunited.
  3. Drive to The Rusty Anchor.
  4. Buy a round of drinks, then find Lucia out on the deck.
  5. After a cutscene, go back to Lucia. Brian and the others arrive.
  6. Mingle until the game moves you on.
  7. Find Donnie in the bathroom, where some military guys are hassling him.
  8. Lucia asks you to dance.
  9. Talk to Rhonda. Her pet lizard has got out: use Focus to spot it in a bush across the street.
  10. Talk to Mattia when he arrives, then play pool with him.
  11. The military guys start taunting you. Take it outside and beat D-Man in a short fistfight.
  12. The police arrive. Follow Lucia as you run.

Choices

  • Errands: you can skip them, but they affect how Lucia feels about Jason.
  • Dance with Lucia or not. Saying yes gives an early boost to your relationship.
  • Accept or turn down Donnie's offer of cocaine.

Tips

  • Take Jason's own car to Andi's bar so you can put Lucia's clothes in the trunk.
  • At the grocery store you only need a few things. The game tells you when you have enough.
  • Mission pay so far should cover the necklace. If not, rob a store.
