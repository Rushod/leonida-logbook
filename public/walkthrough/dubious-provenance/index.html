GTA 6 Dubious Provenance walkthrough

Updated November 21, 2026 · All missions

Dubious Provenance is mission 4 of 26 in Chapter 3: Tits Ass Drug Money. Here's every step, the choices you can make and what to watch out for.

Chapter3
Mission4 / 26

Where it starts

The bag in the closet of the Bayside apartment.

Step by step

  1. Go to the antique store in Capri, on the far west of the map. Try the front door and you're taken upstairs.
  2. Meet Evelyn Andrews and Benedict Halifax.
  3. Break into Evelyn Andrews Antiques. Kick the door in, or go quietly: take the stairs round the back, drop onto the lower roof and climb in through the window above the air conditioner. Let Jason in, then check the desk drawer in the north corner.
  4. Look for Halifax at the motel next door. Talk to a resident, then knock on the upstairs door on the west side.
  5. Go back to Evelyn at the store. She gives you Maryam's address in northwest Capri.
  6. Halifax runs for a boat. Take a Seashark, board his boat, swim ashore and follow him on a bike when he steals a golf cart.
  7. He pepper-sprays Jason and you switch to Lucia. Jump out, use Focus and shoot him in the leg.
  8. Walk up to Halifax so he gives up, then call Jason on Lucia's phone.

Choices

  • Give Halifax the statue: he hands over his Crowex Octane "Bobby Ambrose" watch (wear it from a wardrobe under Accessories > Watches) and the story with him continues in two later parts.
  • Refuse: an optional task appears to call Halifax and give it to him later. If you never do, it vanishes at the end of Chapter 3 and you miss parts 2 and 3.
  • Trusting Halifax at the motel is what lets you play part 3.
  • At the end, shoot him or let him go.

Tips

  • Use the back window if you want to get in quietly.

After the mission

Loot about $2,500 in jewelry and cash from the storage container, and take the '32 Hustler parked there to keep or sell.
