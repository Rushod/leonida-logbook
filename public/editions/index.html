GTA 6 Standard vs Ultimate edition

Updated November 11, 2026

GTA VI comes in two editions. Both include the full game. The Ultimate Edition costs $20 more and adds in-game vehicles, weapons, clothing and properties.

The pre-order bonus comes with both

The Vintage Vice City Pack is included with either edition if you buy by launch day, November 19. It contains a two-tone '55 Vapid Stanier, the Shore Court Garage, and two outfits and hairstyles each for Jason and Lucia.

Which one fits you

  • Standard is the whole story and open world. Nothing in the Ultimate list is described as required to finish the game.
  • Ultimate suits players who want extra cars, clothing and properties from the first hour instead of earning or buying them in the game.

We have not found an announced way to upgrade from Standard to Ultimate later, so check the store page before assuming you can.

Boxed copies

Boxed copies contain a download code and no disc. They need the same 212 GB download as the digital version. See the preload guide.
