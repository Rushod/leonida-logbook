GTA 6 beginner tips: 20 things to know first

Updated November 24, 2026

GTA VI changes a lot of habits from GTA V. Cash on you can vanish, police aren't on the minimap, and stolen cars don't stay yours. These are the tips that save the most trouble in your first hours.

Money

  1. Bank your cash. You lose what you carry if you die or get arrested. Hide it under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom until you get BuckMe, then use ATMs. How BuckMe works.
  2. Visit an ATM before anything risky. It's the only way to deposit.
  3. Fence loot fast. Loot bags are lost on death too. Where to sell.
  4. Don't grind early. Money is tight in Chapter 1 whatever you do. The story unlocks the bank, tools and better jobs.
  5. Search bodies after a fight for cash, ammo and guns.

Police

  1. Cops aren't on your minimap. They find you through witnesses and cameras.
  2. Change your look to lose them. Swap your car, outfit, hat or glasses. Keep a spare outfit in the trunk.
  3. A Pay 'N' Spray respray stops police recognizing a known car.
  4. Commit crimes at night, when fewer people are around to call it in. See the wanted level guide.

Cars

  1. Scan before you steal. WAiNK (hold D-pad Up, then R1/RB) shows alarms and trackers.
  2. Stolen cars aren't yours until you register them, from Chapter 2. How to register.
  3. Watch your fuel. Cars, planes and helicopters run dry. Gas stations also fix small damage cheaply.

Combat

  1. Use Focus. Press both sticks to slow time and highlight enemies, loot, safe codes and alarmed doors. How to raise it.
  2. Buy your guns. Bought guns come back from any Ammu-Nation's locker after death or arrest; picked-up ones don't. What to buy where.
  3. A yellow hitmarker means they're alive. Shoot limbs to keep your Criminal Profile clean. The worst level, Irredeemable, is permanent.
  4. Get a suppressor from the Key Lento Ammu-Nation for quiet takedowns.

Progress

  1. Save manually, often. There's one autosave and no mission replay. How saving works.
  2. Know the points of no return: Tourist Trap and Checking Out.
  3. Scan and study as you go. Vehicle scans, animal studies and WAiNK landmarks all count toward the 100% list, and nothing on it is missable.
  4. Wait to rob banks until you have the Dye Pack Jammer from Credit Check, or dye packs ruin the cash.
