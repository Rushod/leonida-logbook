Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Leonida Logbook (leonidalogbook.com) handles when you visit.

What the site stores

  • Checklist progress. Your ticks are saved in your own browser's local storage. They are not sent to us, and you can clear them with the Reset button or by clearing your browser's site data.
  • No accounts. The site has no sign-up, and we do not ask for your name, email address or payment details.

Services that receive data when you visit

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect against abuse.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address.

Advertising

The site may show ads served by Google AdSense. When ads are shown, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Affiliate links

Some links go to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. When you follow one of these links, Amazon may set cookies and handles your data under its own privacy notice.

Children

The site covers a game rated for mature audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
