How to register, store and call your car in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

A stolen car disappears once you leave it. To keep one, register it as a personal vehicle: Moses does tracker-free cars from Chapter 2, and Pay 'N' Spray does cars with standard trackers from Chapter 3. Then the Scooter Bros app delivers it wherever you are.

Which cars can you register?

How to register a car

  1. Scan it with WAiNK to check the tracker.
  2. Steal it. The Slim Jim from Moses opens most cars quietly.
  3. Moses: drive into his garage, respray it, then choose Register. He won't take cars stolen right next to his garage, so bring one from further away.
  4. Pay 'N' Spray: lose any police first, drive in, choose Register and pay.

The first registration, during the Moses mission, is free. After that the fee depends on the car's value; some sports cars cost over $20,000.

Garage space

  • Each registered car takes a slot, and you start with few. Sell one or buy a garage when you're full.
  • Buy garages in the Vice City Real Estate app, which unlocks in Chapter 2.
  • Parking a stolen car in a garage stores it but doesn't register it.

Calling your car

  • Chapter 1: call Joey from Coastal Auto Care and he brings Jason's truck to you.
  • From Chapter 2: open the Scooter Bros app, pick a registered car and confirm. It's delivered to the nearest road for a fee.
  • Destroyed or impounded? Scooter Bros can recover registered cars for a higher fee. Unregistered cars are gone for good.

Would rather sell? Moses, Pay 'N' Spray and Randy buy stolen cars, which also counts toward the "Vehicles fenced" line on the 100% list. See how to make money.
