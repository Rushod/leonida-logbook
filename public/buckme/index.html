GTA 6 BuckMe and Family Savings explained

Updated November 23, 2026

BuckMe is the phone bank. Cash on you is easy to lose, your checking account is safer, and Family Savings can never be lost, or taken back out. Hitting savings goals raises your checking limit and unlocks discounts.

Your three kinds of money

Cash on you

Used for small buys under $1,000. Lost if you die or get arrested. Deposit it at an ATM (the BuckMe app has an ATM finder).

Checking account

Pays for anything over $1,000 and for RydeMe. Mostly safe, but dying can still cost you part of it. It has a limit: once full, new payments wait as pending until you spend or save some.

Family Savings

Shared by Jason and Lucia. Move money in with Buck Yourself in the app, from anywhere. It can never be lost, and never withdrawn or spent. It exists to hit the goals below.

Goal figures come from PowerPyx's walkthrough and match other launch-week guides. Check your app if anything differs.

Before you have a bank account

BuckMe arrives with the Buck Me mission in Chapter 1, where you get $100 and move it into savings as a tutorial. Until then, hide cash under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom.

Tips

  • Deposit before anything risky: robberies, chases, big missions.
  • Hit $5,000 in savings before Tourist Trap, the first point of no return. The cash you're carrying is lost there.
  • Only put in what you won't need. There's no undo.
  • Loot bags aren't money yet. Take them to a fence before you get caught.
