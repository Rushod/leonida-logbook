How to fast travel in GTA 6

Updated November 25, 2026

There's no map-menu teleport in GTA VI. You fast travel by calling a Paradise Taxi or booking a RydeMe ride on your phone, then paying $100 to skip the journey.

Paradise Taxi (from the start)

Taxis are available once you finish the first Jason mission, Gone Fishing.

  1. Set a destination marker on the map. It works even on areas you haven't discovered.
  2. Press Up on the D-pad for the phone, go to Contacts and call Paradise Taxi. The cab shows as a white car on the minimap.
  3. Get in and hold L2 / LT to talk to the driver.
  4. Press Triangle / Y to pay $100 and skip the ride.

Taxis take the cash you're carrying. You can also just ride along for the normal fare.

RydeMe (after Buck Me)

RydeMe is the rideshare app. It needs a bank account, so it arrives with the Buck Me mission in Chapter 1 (a few guides say Chapter 2; either way it's early). It charges your BuckMe balance, not your cash.

  1. Set a destination on the map.
  2. Open RydeMe on your phone and book the ride. Longer trips cost more.
  3. Get in when the driver arrives and press Triangle / Y to pay $100 and skip.

If no RydeMe driver is free, call Paradise Taxi instead.

Tips

  • Fast travel, then call your car. Skip across the map, then use the Scooter Bros app to have your own car delivered. See how to register and call your car.
  • Switching characters can move you across the map for free, if your partner is already where you want to be. See how to switch.
  • GTA+ members get premium RydeMe membership.
