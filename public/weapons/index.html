Based on hands-on previews. Every point gets re-tested in the finished game on launch day.

What you can carry

  • Up to two concealed handguns and two visible long guns.
  • Extra weapons are stored in your vehicle's trunk.
  • Walking around with a long gun in view makes people nervous and draws police attention.

Ammu-Nation

  • Weapons you drop are not lost. Collect them from any Ammu-Nation locker or your personal vehicle.
  • Some branches sell weapons unique to their region.

Aiming and Focus

  • Aim assist is light, and you can swap shoulders instantly.
  • Focus slows your surroundings and highlights escape routes, police and vehicle weak points.

Ending a fight without killing

CrosshairMeaning
WhiteA standard hit
YellowThe shot will incapacitate
RedThe shot will kill
  • Shots to the knees or arms can stop someone without killing them.
  • You can shoot a weapon out of someone's hands, or intimidate them into running.
  • Firing near people frightens and disorients them.
