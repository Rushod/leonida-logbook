GTA 6 Criminal Profile explained

Updated November 26, 2026

Your Criminal Profile rates how you commit crimes, from Professional to Irredeemable. Jason and Lucia each have their own. It mostly changes how you earn Focus, but the bottom two levels change how police treat you, and the last one is permanent.

The five levels

Professional

Stealth, no violence, clean getaways. Threaten staff without hurting them, disable alarms, destroy CCTV tapes.

Focus: Stealth takedowns · Police: Normal

Hardened

Putting enemies down without killing them: shots to the legs or shoulders (you'll see a yellow hitmarker).

Focus: Stealth takedowns · Police: Normal

Violent

Killing enemies, headshots, finishing off downed enemies, killing civilians or police.

Focus: Headshots · Police: Normal

Psycho

Killing sprees, killing pets, lots of dead cops and civilians, driving over gravestones.

Focus: Headshots · Police: May shoot on sight, and you can't surrender

Irredeemable

Staying Psycho for a long time with no non-violent actions.

Focus: No passive way · Police: Shoot on sight, and you can't surrender. Permanent.

Can you change it back?

Yes, for the first four. Playing quietly and non-lethally moves you back toward Professional. Irredeemable is permanent for that character, so if you go on rampages, mix in some calm play before it locks.

Why it exists: Rockstar's Rob Nelson calls it GTA VI's take on Red Dead Redemption 2's honour system, built so people react to how you play. What Rockstar said.

How to check it

  • Open the map, press L1 / LB to reach the Jason & Lucia tab and pick a character. The rating is at the top.
  • While playing, hold D-pad Right to show the icon in the bottom-right corner.

Easy ways to stay Professional

  • In robberies, threaten rather than shoot, and knock guns out of guards' hands. Missions like Credit Check and First Impressions reward this with a Professional bonus.
  • Leave dogs alone. Hurting them pushes you toward Psycho fast.
  • Shoot tires and engines in chases, not drivers.

Before launch, previews named the levels Professional, Aggressive, Violent, Psycho and "Shattered". The released game uses the five names above.
