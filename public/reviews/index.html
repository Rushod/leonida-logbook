GTA 6 reviews: what critics say

Updated November 24, 2026

Reviews went live on November 18, 2026, the day before launch. The PS5 version opened with a 99 Metascore from 98 reviews, an early figure that will move as more arrive.

The scores so far

  • Metacritic, PS5: 99 from 98 critic reviews (morning of November 18).
  • Metacritic, Xbox Series X: 99 from 9 reviews.
  • Perfect scores: IGN, VICE, Push Square and Cultured Vultures gave 10/10, and Creative Bloq gave 5/5.

For comparison, GTA IV has a 98 and GTA V a 97.

In one line

Critics love the world, the driving and Jason and Lucia's story. The common complaints are fiddly controls, clunky small guns, slow later chapters and the 30 fps cap. Our full reviews round-up has who said what.

What about bugs?

Players have posted plenty of clips of clipping, falls through the map and cars flying after crashes, and PlayStation Network had two outages in launch week. The first post-launch update, 01.002.002, started rolling out on November 23, and Rockstar's notes list seven fixes, among them crashes, autosave and story phone calls, but no performance changes. See the patch notes and what's been reported and what to do.

Where to check the latest scores

Check the refund rules for the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store now. Stores generally treat a pre-order differently once the game is released and downloaded.
