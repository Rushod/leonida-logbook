How GTA VI works

What press previews have reported about the new systems. Every section gets re-tested against the real game on launch day.

The wanted level

Six stars is the maximum
Hollow stars: police know about the crime, but not who did it

You only get stars when someone sees the crime, a victim reports it, or a security system catches it. Police build a description of your face, outfit, weapons and vehicle. Change clothes or hair, drop the weapon they identified, wear a mask, or split up from your partner to shake it.

Two leads, one account

  • Switch between Jason and Lucia at any time, in free roam and in missions.
  • Cash goes into a shared account. Deposit it at an ATM.
  • If one of them is Wasted in a shared fight, both are out.

Stealing cars

  • Older cars: use a slim jim or smash a window.
  • Modern cars need a key cloner or a phone hacking tool.
  • The WAINK phone scanner shows a car's locks, value, alarm and tracker.
  • Garages start with three slots and can be expanded.

Weapons

  • You carry two concealed handguns and two visible long guns. The weapon wheel is gone.
  • Extra guns are stored in your vehicle.
  • Ammu-Nation lockers give back weapons you dropped.
  • Focus slows time for aiming and highlights escape routes.

Making money

  • Every store in Leonida can be robbed.
  • Bigger scores include jewelry stores, small banks and gang compounds.
  • Stolen goods must be sold to a fence.

Getting around

  • Fast travel uses the RydeMe taxi app, buses and trains.
  • Pay & Spray is back, along with Mod Shops and nitrous.
  • Police do not normally show on the minimap during a chase.

Things to do

  • Pool, mini-golf, kayaking, scuba diving and parachuting.
  • Hunting, fishing and shipwreck treasure hunting.
  • Off-road and monster truck racing, gym workouts and a zoo.

The map: state of Leonida

The map is reported to be about three times the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2, with unexplored areas hidden until you visit. The story opens in the Leonida Keys.
