If you played GTA V, a lot works differently in GTA VI. This page sums up the ten biggest changes in one table, with a link to the full guide for each, plus tips for your first hours.

Tips for your first hours

  1. Bank your cash. Getting arrested costs you the money you are carrying. Deposit it at an ATM before anything risky.
  2. Mask up before a robbery. Cameras and witnesses cannot identify a masked face, so you may get hollow stars instead of solid ones.
  3. Register cars you want to keep. Stolen cars vanish unless you register them at Pay & Spray. High-end cars need their tracker removed at a fence first.
  4. Watch your fuel. Cars and bikes can run dry, which is the worst thing that can happen in a chase.
  5. Keep long guns out of sight. Walking around with a rifle on show makes people nervous and draws police.
  6. Use your trunk as an armory. You carry only four guns. Store spares and outfits in your car.
  7. Change clothes to lose the police. Police chase a description. A new outfit, haircut or car can break it.
  8. Train three days in a row. One gym session does nothing for strength. Plan for three consecutive in-game days.
