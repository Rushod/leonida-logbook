What time does GTA 6 unlock?

Updated November 11, 2026

GTA VI releases on Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The exact unlock time for each region is still reported differently by different outlets.

What has been reported

  • Midnight local time. Some coverage says the game unlocks at midnight on November 19 in each region, which would make New Zealand the first country to play.
  • Not fully confirmed. Other coverage, updated on November 10, says Rockstar has not published a region-by-region schedule yet.

Until Rockstar posts a full schedule, treat any site that lists exact times for every country as a guess. This page will be updated as soon as official times are published.

How to check your own unlock time

The store on your console is the most reliable source, because it shows the time for your account and region.

  • PS5: open GTA VI from your Game Library or the PlayStation Store. Pre-ordered games show a countdown on the game's page.
  • Xbox Series X|S: open GTA VI in the Microsoft Store on your console and check the release date and time on its page.

Does preloading let you play early?

No. Preloading only downloads the game ahead of time. It stays locked until the unlock time for your region.
