GTA 6 100% completion checklist

Updated November 21, 2026

GTA VI's 100% list has 35 requirements in three groups. Most only ask for part of a category, nothing on it is missable, and finishing it unlocks the A Long Road trophy. Tick each line off as you go.

Requirements35
MissableNone
Rough timeAbout 200 hours

How it works

  • The list unlocks after the story. Everything you did before still counts. Find it under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion.
  • The story is half the score. Finishing the story is worth about 50%. The other eleven Story & Tasks lines add about a quarter, and each exploration or discovery line is worth about 1%.
  • It's lenient. It works out at roughly 60% of everything in the game. Most stranger missions and side quests aren't required, and you only study animals, not hunt them.
  • Start scanning early. Vehicle scans, animal studies and WAiNK locations pile up naturally while you play the story.

Where to start

  1. Play the story with the walkthrough, scanning cars and studying animals along the way.
  2. Rob as you go. Bank and payday loan jobs open after Credit Check in Chapter 3. Fencing the loot covers four more lines. See the robbery guide.
  3. Do the all-or-nothing lines: 42 shooting range golds, 42 drone trials, 33 underwater discoveries, 15 target decks and all 8 vaults.
  4. Finish the partial lines last and pick the easiest ones: you only need 300 of 397 stunts and 70 of 80 challenges.
