How to make money in GTA 6

Updated November 26, 2026

Money is tight early, and the story asks you to save it (you need $15,000 before leaving Chapter 2). Here are the best ways to earn at each stage, and how not to lose what you've got.

Keep it safe first

Cash on you is lost if you're arrested or die, and the story takes it from you at Tourist Trap. Move money into BuckMe savings as soon as you have an account (from the Buck Me mission).

Chapter 1

  • Story missions. Payment lands in the mailbox outside Jason's house.
  • Stick-ups. Rob convenience, fast food and liquor store registers. Small money, light police response. Some have a back-room safe: kick the door, make the clerk open it, or look for the code on a sticky note.
  • Drugs. Sell what you find to Brian at his lock-up and, after Clean Up After Donnie, at Donnie's trailer. Cocaine and crystal pay far more than weed.
  • Treasure. Sell gold doubloons from shipwrecks to Kenton (from Island Dreams).

Chapter 2

  • Sell cars to Moses. About $1,000 to $5,000 each, tracker-free only. He has a cooldown after each sale; sleeping at your safehouse resets it.
  • Moses's special car jobs (from Daily Driver).
  • The four Coffee Break jobs, including Hit Avery Pawn and Hit Kingpin Bowling.
  • Pawn shops and jewelry stores. Better money, tougher security. Go in the back to cut the alarm and destroy the CCTV tape.

Chapter 3 and later

  • Gang hideouts. A yellow icon appears when you're near one; check the map for what's inside first. One of the best earners.
  • Randy's supercars. The first one pays $21,250, plus $10,000 if it's undamaged. Then work through his wish list. See Steal the Zorrusso.
  • Banks and payday loan stores, once you have the Dye Pack Jammer from Credit Check. The biggest regular payouts, and the fastest police response, so plan your escape.
  • Yasmani (from Boobie Time) pays the most for jewelry and marked bills.
  • Bank vaults open late in Chapter 4 and are the largest scores in free roam.
