GTA 6 fence locations: where to sell stolen goods

Updated November 24, 2026

Narcotics, jewelry and marked bills are worth nothing until a fence buys them. The best are in Vice City from Chapter 3: Tyrone for narcotics, Yasmani for jewelry and marked bills.

Car buyers

  • Moses, Port Gellhorn: tracker-free cars, from Chapter 2.
  • Pay 'N' Spray: also buys stolen cars.
  • Randy, Balsero (Vice City): high-end cars, including ones with advanced trackers, from Steal the Zorrusso.

See how to register cars if you'd rather keep one.

How fencing works

  • Once you've met a fence, they show on the map as a green icon with their initials. Hover over it to see their rates before you drive over.
  • Early in Chapter 1 you're paid in cash, left in the mailbox outside Jason's house. Once you have BuckMe, fences pay into your account.
  • From Chapter 3 you can sell from your garage and pick the fence.
  • Your partner can fence for you: put the bag in your car's trunk and tell them to Fence Goods (the option doesn't always show).
  • If your partner wouldn't hand over a bag so you could sell it, update the game. Patch 01.002.002 fixed that on November 23 (patch notes).
  • Loot bags are lost if you die, so sell big scores straight away.

Fencing fills four lines of the 100% list: narcotics and jewelry ($100,000 each), marked bills ($250,000) and vehicles ($200,000).
