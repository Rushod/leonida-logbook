What counts toward 100%

100% completion in GTA VI is built from scans, studies and activities around Leonida. The trophy column comes from the trophy list, as reported by GamesRadar+ and GTABase on November 18. The 100% and total columns come from GTABase's completion guide only, so treat them as early figures.

Other things to find

  • Underwater discoveries. All of them count toward 100%, according to GTABase. Shipwrecks can be searched for treasure.
  • Wildlife. Every species can be studied for a checklist. See wildlife.
  • Hidden areas. Unexplored parts of the map stay hidden until you visit them.
  • Hideouts. In-game social media posts can reveal them.

How long does 100% take?

GTABase estimates about 200 hours for the platinum trophy, which needs 100% completion. See the full trophy list news and the story length guide.
