GTA 6 collectibles and hidden items

Updated November 11, 2026

Previews have not described a traditional collectibles set yet. Here is what is known about things to find, and how this page will work at launch.

Locations at launchCollectibles are added with map positions as players find them.

Things to find, from previews

  • Shipwreck treasure. Underwater wrecks can be searched for treasure.
  • Wildlife. Every species can be studied for a checklist. See wildlife.
  • Hidden areas. Unexplored parts of the map stay hidden until you visit them.
  • Hideouts. In-game social media posts can reveal them.
  • Wearable finds. You can pick up and wear items from the world, such as sunglasses dropped in a fight.

How this page will work

Each collectible gets a location on the interactive map and a tick box in the progress tracker. Nothing is listed until it has been found in the game.
