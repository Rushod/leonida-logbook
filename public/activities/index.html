What is not a full mini-game

Previews say tennis, surfing and skateboarding are not included as full activities.

More to do in Leonida

  • Robberies. Every store can be robbed, with bigger scores at jewelry stores, small banks and gang compounds. See the robbery guide.
  • Time together. Dates and shared activities improve how well Jason and Lucia work together during robberies.
  • Random events. Examples from previews include a stranger daring you to jump off a bridge and a suspect escaping from a police car.
  • Livestream events. In-game social media shows events as they happen, and you can travel to where the streamer is.
  • Eating out. Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell have drive-thrus, or you can walk inside.
