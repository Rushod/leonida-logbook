How long is GTA 6?

Updated November 11, 2026

About 80 hours for the main story, going by one Rockstar developer's own playthrough. That is far longer than GTA V.

Where the 80 hours comes from

Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told The New York Times that his playthrough in February 2026 took about 80 hours. It covered the main story and some optional goals that affect the narrative. It did not include all the side content, which would add more.

How the story is built

  • The story is split into chapters, and previews describe its length as comparable to Red Dead Redemption 2.
  • It opens in the Leonida Keys, and the rest of the map opens up after the first missions.
  • Previews say missions are less restrictive than in GTA V, with more than one way to approach them.

How many missions are there?

Rockstar has not published a mission count. The progress tracker gets the full mission list once the game is out and it can be verified.

How long for 100%?

Unknown until players finish it. With a map about three times the size of Red Dead Redemption 2's, expect far more than 80 hours. See how big the map is.
