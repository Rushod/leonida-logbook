About Leonida Logbook

An independent fan-made companion for Grand Theft Auto VI.

Leonida Logbook is built for players who want quick, checked answers: when the game unlocks, how its systems work, what is left to find, and which cheat codes are real.

How information is checked

Before launch, pages are based on official announcements and press previews, and each page says so. From launch day, guides, cheat codes and checklists are tested against the game before they are listed. Nothing is published from leaks or rumor lists.

Who runs it

The site is run by an independent fan. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

How the site is funded

The site earns money from advertising and from affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. This does not change what you pay.
