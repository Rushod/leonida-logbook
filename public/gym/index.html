GTA 6 gym guide: how workouts work

Updated November 11, 2026

Gym workouts in GTA VI are short sessions of three exercises with timing inputs, and strength only improves if you train three days in a row.

Based on hands-on previews. Every point gets re-tested in the finished game on launch day.

How a workout works

  • A session is three exercises, tracked on a circular indicator.
  • Squats and pull-ups use rhythm or timing inputs on the controller triggers.
  • Poor timing drains extra stamina.
  • Gyms have changing rooms and vending machines.

How to gain strength

Strength goes up after training on three consecutive in-game days. A single session is not enough.

Food and items that help

ItemEffect
Protein barsBoost workout gains
Energy drinksBoost stamina
PainkillersHelp less each time you repeat them

Fighting styles

Lucia trains in kickboxing and mixed martial arts. Jason fights in a rougher street style.
