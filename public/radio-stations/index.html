GTA 6 radio stations and hosts

Updated November 11, 2026

Rockstar has revealed six GTA VI radio stations so far, with previews on its official music page. The full song list has not been published.

The six stations

StationGenreReported hosts
Flash FMPopRobyn and Alex Consani
Cocoteo FMModern Latin: reggaeton, dembow, bachata, merengueBad Bunny and RaiNao

Station names and genres come from Rockstar's reveal in October 2026. Host names are as reported by games media at the time.

New ways to listen

  • On foot. Jason and Lucia can listen through earbuds connected to their phones, not only in vehicles.
  • Podcasts on demand. The radio menu has a podcast section you can play whenever you like.
  • Streamer-safe music. Previews describe a toggle for copyright-free music, made for people who record or stream.

Is the full soundtrack out?

No. Rockstar has not published a track list, and more stations are expected. Song lists you see before launch are guesses or leaks.
